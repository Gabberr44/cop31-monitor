# Opening plenary of COP31 / CMP21 / CMA8
✅ Final version — consolidated on 10/11/2026 at 07:30 (Italian time)

**When:** 09/11 (Monday), morning, exact time n/a ✅ [1]; started about 90 minutes late according to AFP 🟡 [3] · **Where:** Antalya EXPO Center, Antalya, Türkiye [1][2] · **Organiser:** UNFCCC Secretariat (the UN Framework Convention on Climate Change) and COP31 Presidency (Türkiye)
**Speakers:** **Murat Kurum** (COP31 President, Minister of Environment, Urbanisation and Climate Change, Türkiye) [2]; **Chris Bowen** (Australian Minister for Climate Change and Energy, President of Negotiations) [2][4]; **Simon Stiell** (Executive Secretary, UNFCCC) [2][4]; **António Guterres** (Secretary-General, UN) [3]; **André Corrêa do Lago** (COP30 President, Brazil; opened the session) [3]
🎥 Video not yet available

Acronyms in the title: COP31 is the 31st UN climate conference; CMP21 is the 21st meeting of the Parties to the Kyoto Protocol; CMA8 is the 8th meeting of the Parties to the Paris Agreement.

## In brief
**COP31** opened in Antalya under a split presidency, the first in COP history: **Türkiye** hosts and presides over the conference and the Action Agenda, while **Australia** leads the negotiations [2][3][4]. **The Presidency** calls it the "Implementation COP" ✅ [2]; the agenda adopted will shape future climate-data requests to SMEs.

Source note: the delay of about 90 minutes comes from a single wire (AFP), consistent in content with Climate Home [4].

## Key points
- **Developing countries** got the climate finance work programme created at COP30 onto the agenda, after talks ran into Sunday night 🟡 [3][4]. The agenda was adopted without a floor fight 🟡 [3][4].
  - **The African Group** had asked for separate items on tripling adaptation finance and on trade: these requests were shelved 🟡 [3][4]. Adaptation will be handled in other strands, such as the GGA (the Global Goal on Adaptation) and the finance work programme 🟡 [3][4].
  - **Many developing countries** backed the African proposals, while **the European Union and Canada** opposed them, according to AP 🟡 [7].
  - **The European Union** argued that COP30 had not created a separate agenda item for the finance work programme, according to Down To Earth (Tier C) [8]. The programme covers implementation of Paris Agreement Art. 9.1 [8].
  - **Nepal** had requested an item on mountains: it was not adopted and the topic moves to consultations, according to Climate Home ⚠️ [4]. Nepal said the compromise "falls short of our expectations"; single source ⚠️ [4].
  - **The Presidency consultations**, according to Down To Earth (Tier C), move Green Climate Fund funding to an existing item ⚠️ [8]. The seventh review of the financial mechanism is deferred to COP32 ⚠️ [8]. Mountains will be considered for a research dialogue at SBSTA 66 (the Subsidiary Body for Scientific and Technological Advice) ⚠️ [8]. The subsidiary-body chairs will invite submissions for the next trade dialogue in Bonn ⚠️ [8]. The deferral of the mountains item is consistent with Climate Home [4].
- **Murat Kurum, COP31 President**, called for an "Implementation COP" ✅ [2]. He said the 1.5 °C goal "remains our guide" and that "our promises must be backed by capital" ✅ [2]. **Kurum** also said this is "the first COP where it is clear beyond a reasonable doubt that the world will overshoot the 1.5-degree target", according to AP 🟡 [7].
- **Simon Stiell, UNFCCC Executive Secretary**, said progress is not fast enough 🟡 [4][5]. **Stiell** cited USD 265 bn saved thanks to renewables in the first half of 2026, according to the Presidency release ✅ [2].
- **Chris Bowen, President of Negotiations**, said the final "cover decision" will not be "a lengthy political declaration" 🟡 [4]. It will instead be a "focused, practical, concrete" response 🟡 [4]. The day before he had said "there will be no surprises"; there is a single Tier B source for the quotes 🟡 [4].
- **António Guterres, UN Secretary-General**, urged leaders to "get serious" about emissions, according to AFP 🟡 [3].

## Announcements and numbers
- **The COP31 Presidency** proposes the voluntary "35-by-35" goal: electricity at 35% of final energy consumption by 2035 ✅ [2][6]. The current share is about 20%, according to AFP 🟡 [3].
- **The Türkiye, Australia, Brazil and Azerbaijan presidencies** published the "Belém Mission to 1.5" report ✅ [2].
- **The COP31 Presidency** recalls the Baku goal: at least USD 300 bn a year for developing countries by 2035 and USD 1.3 trillion a year from all sources ✅ [2].
- **The WMO (the World Meteorological Organization)**, cited by the Presidency, says each of the last 12 years ranks among the warmest on record, with the last four the warmest of all ✅ [2].
- **Germany** provided €4.7 bn in climate finance in 2025, short of its €6 bn target, according to Climate Home ⚠️ [4].
- **Egypt and Argentina** submitted 2035 NDCs (national emission-reduction plans); single source ⚠️ [4].
- **The United States** sent no administration officials 🟡 [3][5]. **A US congressional delegation** will attend, according to AFP 🟡 [3].
- **The organisers** asked those without essential business to collect badges on Tuesday, due to long queues at registration, according to AP 🟡 [7]. About 68,500 people registered 🟡 [7].

## Quotes
- "Now our promises must be backed by capital." — **Murat Kurum, COP31 President** [2]
- "We have entered a new age of climate extremes." — **António Guterres, UN Secretary-General**, per AFP [3]

## Relevance for the VSME
**Medium** — **The plenary** took no reporting decisions. However, putting the climate finance work programme on the agenda and moving adaptation into other strands will shape future requests for physical-risk and transition data that banks and customers send to SMEs.
