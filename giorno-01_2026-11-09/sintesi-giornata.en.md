# COP31 — Day 1 · Monday 9 November 2026
✅ Final version — consolidated on 10/11/2026 at 07:30 (Italian time)

## In brief
- **What happened:** **COP31 (the 31st UN climate conference)** opened in Antalya and adopted its agenda without a floor fight, including the climate finance work programme 🟡 [10][11].
- **Why it matters:** The finance work programme on the agenda and the shift of adaptation to other strands will shape the physical-risk and transition data that banks and customers request from SMEs.
- **Tomorrow:** **The "Energy and Transport" day on Tuesday 10 Nov** features the Baku High-Level Dialogue on Adaptation and a workshop on non-market approaches ✅ [1].

## Key facts
1. **Developing countries** got the climate finance work programme created at COP30 onto the agenda 🟡 [10][11]. Talks ran into Sunday night 🟡 [10][11]. **The African Group** had asked for separate items on tripling adaptation finance and on trade: these requests were shelved 🟡 [10][11]. Adaptation will be handled under the GGA (the Global Goal on Adaptation) and the finance work programme 🟡 [10][11]. **The opening plenary** started about 90 minutes late, according to the AFP news agency 🟡 [10].
2. **Murat Kurum, COP31 President and Türkiye's Environment Minister**, opened the "Implementation COP" ✅ [3]. He said the 1.5 °C goal "remains our guide" and "our promises must be backed by capital" ✅ [3]. **Chris Bowen, Australia's Minister for Climate Change and Energy**, leads the negotiations on behalf of **Australia** 🟡 [11]. He promises a "focused, practical, concrete" final decision rather than a lengthy political declaration 🟡 [11].
3. **Simon Stiell, Executive Secretary of the UNFCCC (the UN Framework Convention on Climate Change)**, said progress is "nowhere near fast enough" 🟡 [9][11]. **Stiell** noted that renewables saved USD 265 bn in the first half of 2026, according to the Presidency ✅ [3]. **António Guterres, UN Secretary-General**, said the world has entered "a new age of climate extremes", according to AFP 🟡 [10].
4. **The COP31 Presidency** relaunched its voluntary "35-by-35" goal: electricity at 35% of final consumption by 2035 ✅ [3]. **The Presidency**, with Australia, Brazil and Azerbaijan, published the "Belém Mission to 1.5" report ✅ [3]. **Reuters and Climate Home** report criticism: the goal does not require the additional electricity to be low-carbon 🟡 [9][12].
5. **The COP31 Presidency, WHO (the World Health Organization) and ATACH (the Alliance for Transformative Action on Climate and Health)** launched the Antalya Initiative for Resilient Health Systems (AIR) ✅ [3]. The launch took place on the "Food, Agriculture and Health" day ✅ [3]. **Health Policy Watch** writes that the initiative is voluntary and has no funding or participating countries yet; single source ⚠️ [19].
6. **The UNFCCC Secretariat** held the Art. 6.2 Ambition Dialogue, 15:00–18:00 Antalya (13:00–16:00 Italian time) ✅ [7]. The topic was the authorisation of ITMOs (emission reductions traded between countries) ✅ [7]. There is no written account of the discussion.
7. **The United States** sent no administration officials 🟡 [9][10]. **The AP news agency** reports that **the European Union and Canada** opposed the African Group's agenda proposals 🟡 [25]. **AP** counts about 68,500 registered participants, with queues at accreditation 🟡 [25].

**Context and other news**
- **CARE, an NGO**, in an analysis published by Climate Home, finds that developing countries' climate-loan repayments nearly tripled ⚠️ [27]. They rose from USD 6.9 bn (2020) to USD 20.1 bn (2024) and could exceed new lending by 2036 ⚠️ [27].
- **The UNEP Adaptation Gap Report**, cited by Climate Home, estimates that tripled finance would reach USD 85–120 bn a year, against needs of USD 305 bn [12].
- **Reuters** reports that a glacier collapse in Nepal killed more than 1,450 people 🟡 [9].
- **Carbon Brief** published a Q&A on the Belém-Antalya Mechanism for a just transition today [15].
- **Ireland and Tuvalu** will co-chair the second conference on transitioning away from fossil fuels, according to Climate Home ⚠️ [11]. The main event will be held in late July in Tuvalu ⚠️ [11].
- **The IPCC (the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change)** remains deadlocked over the timing of the AR7 (seventh assessment cycle) reports, according to Climate Home ⚠️ [11]. Virtual talks start on 1 December ⚠️ [11].
- **GRI (the Global Reporting Initiative, the reporting-standards body)** will take part in six events on climate reporting and transition plans between 11 and 17 Nov ✅ [8].
