# COP31 — Day 3 · Wednesday 11 November 2026
✅ Final version — consolidated on 12/11/2026 at 07:45 (Italian time). First evening version: 11/11/2026 at 21:30 (Italian time).

## In brief
- **What happened:** **World leaders** opened the Antalya Leaders' Summit, on the day devoted to the "Zero Waste" theme ✅ [3][4][5].
- **Why it matters:** **António Guterres, UN Secretary-General**, recalls that even with NDCs (national emission-reduction plans) implemented the world exceeds 2.5 °C ✅ [1].
- **Tomorrow:** **Leaders** close the Leaders' Summit with the last national statements and the closing ceremony ✅ [18].

## Key facts
1. **António Guterres, UN Secretary-General**, proposes a "climate justice compact" with four pillars ✅ [1]
   - **The four pillars** are: transition away from fossil fuels through national plans that protect workers and communities; protection of the most vulnerable; affordable climate finance; cooperation against trade and technology barriers ✅ [1]
   - **Guterres** calls for taxing fossil fuel companies' windfall profits ✅ [1]
   - **Guterres** recalls that the nine largest fossil fuel companies earned USD 95 bn in one quarter ✅ [1]
   - **The loss and damage fund** holds only about USD 600 m ✅ [1]
   - **Guterres**: "Fossil fuels must go the way of the dinosaurs" ✅ [1][8]
   - **Guterres**: "The climate crisis is outrunning us" 🟡 [8][16]
2. **Guterres** recalls that, even with NDCs implemented, the world will exceed 2.5 °C by the end of the century ✅ [1]
   - **Reuters and Al Jazeera/AFP** report record 2025 emissions and the last four years as the hottest 🟡 [7][8]
3. **Recep Tayyip Erdoğan, President of Türkiye**, acknowledges that three decades of negotiations have not delivered enough ✅ [1]. **Erdoğan** calls for more support for developing countries ✅ [1]
   - **The COP31 Presidency (Türkiye)** promotes the "Climate Implementation Bridge" and "35-by-35" ✅ [2]
   - **The COP31 Presidency** names the 1.5 °C goal as the "compass" ✅ [2]
   - **Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, President of Brazil, and Erdoğan** warn that wars and tariff fights must not distract from climate action 🟡 [7][16]
   - **Lula**: "Climate change disappeared from the speeches and documents from global leadership" 🟡 [7][16]
4. **Ursula von der Leyen, President of the European Commission**, backs "35-by-35" (the global electrification pledge) and invites other countries to join, according to Reuters and Politico (as reported by Carbon Brief) 🟡 [7][14]
   - **Climate Home News and a diplomat quoted by Politico** count only about a dozen endorsing countries 🟡 [5][14]
   - **Germany, Norway and Ethiopia** are among the supporters 🟡 [5][17]
   - **The European Union** has not formally signed yet, according to Climate Home News (single source) ⚠️ [5]
   - **India** is considering staying out, according to ABC News Australia ⚠️ [17]
5. **Lula** announces national roadmaps to reduce fossil fuel dependence and end deforestation ✅ [1]
   - **Han Zheng, Vice President of China**, says developed countries should lead cuts and honour finance commitments, according to Reuters ⚠️ [7]
6. **The UN Early Warnings for All initiative** counts 136 countries (70%) with multi-hazard early warning systems, 17 more than in March 2025 ✅ [15]
   - **The report** finds disaster mortality 6.1 times lower where systems are strong ✅ [15]
   - **One country in three** still has no early warning system ✅ [15]
   - **Guterres** asks UNEP (the UN Environment Programme) for a global "near-zero" methane standard for oil and gas ✅ [1]
   - **The "Zero Waste" Leaders' Roundtable** proposed raising circular material use to at least 15% by 2035 ✅ [4]
7. **France, Germany, Italy, Japan and Canada** did not send their leaders 🟡 [8][16]. **The United States** is absent for the second year 🟡 [8][16]
   - **Carbon Brief** calculates that developed countries' public climate finance pledges drop by more than 70% ⚠️ [10]
