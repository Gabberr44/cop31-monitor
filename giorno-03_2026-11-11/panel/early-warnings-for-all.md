## EN
**Context.** High-level event on the Early Warnings for All initiative, with the release of a progress report [15].

**Key points**
- ✅ 136 countries (70%) now have multi-hazard early warning systems, 17 more than in March 2025 [15].
- ✅ Where systems are substantial or comprehensive, disaster mortality is 6.1 times lower than in countries with limited systems [15].
- ✅ About one country in three still has no system; the biggest gaps are in least developed countries, small islands and conflict-affected countries [15].
- ✅ Guterres's three priorities: make early warning a permanent public service; close finance and capacity gaps; move from fragmented projects to coordinated, long-term support for national plans [15].
- ✅ WMO warns the current El Niño could exceed existing monitoring capacity [15].

**Announcements / commitments / numbers**
- ✅ About USD 2 bn mobilised since 2022; about 160 countries receiving support [15].
- ✅ In 2025, 146 anticipatory action activations in 54 countries reached 9.6 million people [15].

**Quotes**
- "No one should lose their life simply because a warning came too late – or never came at all." — António Guterres [15]

**Relevance for reporting / VSME: Medium.** No obligations, but public warning and hazard data are the basis for assessing the physical risk that banks and insurers increasingly ask SMEs about.
