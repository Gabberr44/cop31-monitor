# "Zero Waste" day: Leaders' Roundtable on zero waste, circularity and methane

**Scheduled speakers [2]:** **Emine Erdoğan** (First Lady of Türkiye, Chair of the UN Secretary-General's Advisory Board on Zero Waste); **Murat Kurum** (President, COP31); **Samed Ağırbaş** (High-Level Climate Champion, COP31); **António Guterres** (Secretary-General, UN)

## In brief
**The COP31 Presidency** placed zero waste, circularity and methane on the conference's Action Agenda ✅ [2]. **The 90-minute roundtable** invited heads of state to back shared goals and announce national commitments ✅ [2].

## Key points
- **The Presidency's concept note** proposes halving the projected increase in global municipal solid waste by 2035 ✅ [2]
- **The same note** proposes raising the circular material use rate in production to at least 15% by 2035 ✅ [2]
- **António Guterres, UN Secretary-General**, asked UNEP (the UN Environment Programme) to develop a global standard for "near-zero" methane emissions in oil and gas ✅ [1]
- **IMEO (UNEP's International Methane Emissions Observatory)** has sent over 5,000 satellite alerts since 2023, but only one in eight got a response ✅ [1]. **Monitoring** helped cut emissions in 45 cases in 13 countries ✅ [1]
- **UN News** recalls that methane traps over 80 times more heat than CO₂ over 20 years ✅ [1]. **Methane** is responsible for nearly a third of warming to date ✅ [1]
- **Türkiye's single-use plastics ban** still awaits the president's signature, according to Climate Home ⚠️ [3][4]
- **Outcome of the roundtable (endorsements, national commitments):** no written account found at 21:30 (Italian time).

## Announcements and numbers
- **A pipeline leak in Iraq**, lasting two years, emitted as much as 350,000 cars ✅ [1]
- **Türkiye** recycles 12% of its waste (Eurostat, 2020) versus about 50% in the European Union, according to Climate Home ⚠️ [4]
- **The Sincan landfill** leaks about 1,200 kg of methane per hour, according to a Climate Home estimate based on Carbon Mapper data ⚠️ [4]

_**Climate Home News** discloses its travel and accommodation were paid by the Turkish government._

## Quotes
- "our climate emergency brake" — **Julia Ferini, UNEP IMEO**, describing methane according to UN News; the phrase is reported in paraphrased form [1]
