# Opening of the Antalya Leaders' Summit and leaders' statements
✅ Final version — consolidated on 12/11/2026 at 07:45 (Italian time). First evening version: 11/11/2026 at 21:30 (Italian time).

**When:** 11/11, opening session 12:15–13:30 Antalya (10:15–11:30 Italian time); national statements in the afternoon [3] · **Where:** Plenary Hall-1 (Anadolu), Antalya EXPO Center [3] · **Organiser:** COP31 Presidency (Türkiye) and UNFCCC (the UN climate convention)
**Speakers (quoted by sources):** **António Guterres** (Secretary-General, UN) [1][5][6]; **Recep Tayyip Erdoğan** (President, Türkiye) [1][4][5]; **Anthony Albanese** (Prime Minister, Australia) [4][5]; **Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva** (President, Brazil; speech partly read by minister João Paulo Capobianco, according to Climate Home) [1][4][5]; **Han Zheng** (Vice President, China) [5]; **Ursula von der Leyen** (President, European Commission) [5][7]; **Abiy Ahmed** (Prime Minister, Ethiopia) [4][5]; **Mohamed Muizzu** (Maldives) [5]; **Nataša Pirc Musar** (Slovenia) [5]; **Masoud Pezeshkian** (Iran) [5][6]; **Hilda Heine** (Marshall Islands) [4]; **Prince Abdulaziz bin Salman** (Saudi Arabia) [4]

## In brief
**The Leaders' Summit**, COP31's political segment, opened the first of its two days with road closures and tighter security [1][4]. **The COP31 Presidency** opened with a speech on Türkiye as a resilience model and on the "Climate Implementation Bridge" [2].

## Key points
- **António Guterres, UN Secretary-General**, proposes a "climate justice compact" with four pillars ✅ [1]. **Climate Home News** reports the same proposal 🟡 [4]
  - transition away from fossil fuels through national plans that protect workers and communities;
  - protection of the most vulnerable (early warning, adaptation, fast post-disaster support);
  - affordable climate finance;
  - cooperation against trade and technology barriers.
- **Guterres** warns that, even with NDCs (national emission-reduction plans) fully implemented, the world heads for over 2.5 °C by the end of the century ✅ [1]. **Guterres** calls for taxing fossil fuel companies' windfall profits ✅ [1]
  - **Reuters and Al Jazeera/AFP** report record 2025 emissions and the last four years as the hottest 🟡 [5][6]
- **Recep Tayyip Erdoğan, President of Türkiye**, acknowledges that three decades of negotiations have not delivered enough ✅ [1]. **Erdoğan** calls for more support for developing countries ✅ [1]
  - **Erdoğan** did not mention fossil fuels and focused on nature and tree planting, according to Climate Home ⚠️ [4]
- **Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, President of Brazil**, announces national roadmaps to reduce fossil fuel dependence and end deforestation ✅ [1]
  - **Lula** wants the fossil fuel, deforestation and USD 1.3 tn-a-year finance roadmaps included in the next Global Stocktake (the Paris Agreement's global review), according to Climate Home ⚠️ [4]
- **Ursula von der Leyen, President of the European Commission**, backs "35-by-35" (the global electrification pledge) and invites others to join, according to Reuters and Politico (via Carbon Brief) 🟡 [5][9]
  - **Climate Home News** instead writes that the European Union and the UK "welcome" the focus but have not signed up ⚠️ [4]
  - **CAN Europe (NGO network, own position)** argues von der Leyen did not link electrification to a fossil fuel exit ⚠️ [8]
- **France, Germany, Italy, Japan and Canada** did not send their leaders 🟡 [6][10]. **The United States** is absent for the second year 🟡 [6][10]
- **Lula and Erdoğan** warn that wars and tariff fights must not distract from climate action 🟡 [5][10]. **Lula**: "Climate change disappeared from the speeches and documents from global leadership" [10]
- **Anthony Albanese, Prime Minister of Australia**, devotes his speech to Pacific countries and calls for investment in clean energy and adaptation, citing the Pacific Resilience Facility, according to ABC ⚠️ [11]
  - **Australian groups** criticise new coal and gas approvals, according to ABC ⚠️ [11]
- **Climate Home News** counts about 40 leaders listed, with some dropouts (Nigeria, Kenya, Egypt, Netherlands, Sweden) ⚠️ [4]

## Announcements and numbers
- **Guterres** recalls that the nine largest oil companies earned USD 95 bn in one quarter ✅ [1]
- **The loss and damage fund** has received about USD 600 m, "about 14 hours" of those profits, according to Guterres ✅ [1]
- **The COP31 Presidency** confirms Türkiye's net zero target year of 2053 ✅ [2]
- **The COP31 Presidency** presents the "Climate Implementation Bridge" to connect national plans, projects, finance and technology ✅ [2]
- **Von der Leyen** says over 70% of Europe's electricity already comes from clean sources, according to DPA ⚠️ [7]
- **The European Union** aims to bring electricity to 46% of final energy consumption by 2040, according to DPA ⚠️ [7]
- **About a dozen countries** publicly back 35-by-35 🟡 [4][9]. **Germany, Norway and Ethiopia** are among them 🟡 [4][11]
  - **Climate Home News** expects the full list next week and writes that Saudi Arabia is not on board ⚠️ [4]
  - **India** is considering staying out, according to ABC ⚠️ [11]

## Quotes
- "Fossil fuels must go the way of the dinosaurs." — **António Guterres, UN Secretary-General** [1][6]
- "We support the 35-by-35 Global Electrification Pledge, and I encourage others to do the same." — **Ursula von der Leyen, President of the European Commission** (Reuters) [5]

## Relevance for the VSME
