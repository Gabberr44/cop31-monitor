# Climate finance: falling public pledges and the Tropical Forest Forever Facility (TFFF) event
✅ Final version — consolidated on 12/11/2026 at 07:45 (Italian time). First evening version: 11/11/2026 at 21:30 (Italian time).

**When:** 11/11, time n/a · **Where:** Antalya EXPO Center (TFFF event on the sidelines of the Leaders' Summit) [3] · **Organiser:** TFFF event: Brazil and supporting countries, according to Climate Home [3]; analysis: Carbon Brief [4]
**Speakers:** **João Paulo Capobianco** (Environment Minister, Brazil; speaking for President Lula) [3]; no other speaker confirmed by written sources
🎥 Video not yet available

## In brief
**Carbon Brief** calculates that developed countries' public climate finance pledges collapsed in 2026, because most multi-year pledges expired in 2025 ⚠️ [4]. **António Guterres, UN Secretary-General**, calls for "affordable" finance and taxes on fossil fuel companies' windfall profits ✅ [1].

## Key points
- **Carbon Brief** estimates that annual public pledges to developing countries fall by more than 70% compared with previous ones ⚠️ [4]
- **Only five countries** have announced new multi-year pledges, according to Carbon Brief ⚠️ [4]. **The UK** is the only major donor among them ⚠️ [4]
- **Germany and France** have no pledges beyond 2025, according to Carbon Brief ⚠️ [4]. **The United States** will provide no official climate finance ⚠️ [4]. **Australia, COP31 co-president**, has not announced a pledge ⚠️ [4]
- **UN News** reports open disputes in the negotiations on the predictability of climate finance ✅ [1]. **Parties** also remain divided on loss and damage, including Nepal's request after a glacier collapse ✅ [1]
- **The TFFF (the tropical forest fund)** has raised about USD 7.9 bn against a USD 10 bn start-up target, according to Climate Home ⚠️ [3]
  - **Norway** has pledged USD 3 bn, but only if the fund reaches USD 10 bn by year-end ⚠️ [3]
  - **Governments** announced no new pledges at the event; **foundations** put in USD 72 m ⚠️ [3]
- **Zimbabwe** proposes a first-loss guarantee facility for Mission 300 projects, according to Climate Home ⚠️ [3]. **The Solomon Islands** propose a finance window for small islands and least developed countries ⚠️ [3]

## Announcements and numbers
- **Guterres** puts the loss and damage fund at about USD 600 m ✅ [1]
- **The European Union and its member states** provided a record EUR 32.3 bn in 2025, according to Carbon Brief ⚠️ [4]
- **The UK** has pledged GBP 6 bn of aid for 2026-27 to 2028-29, plus GBP 6.7 bn outside the aid budget, according to Carbon Brief ⚠️ [4]
- **Australia, the Pacific Islands and Türkiye** present the Pacific Resilience Facility, "USD 1.5 billion for a 1.5C World", on Thursday 12 Nov: 15:30–17:00 Antalya (13:30–15:00 Italian time) ✅ [2]

## Quotes
- "We cannot demand that countries transform their economies while denying them the means to do so." — **António Guterres, UN Secretary-General** [1]
