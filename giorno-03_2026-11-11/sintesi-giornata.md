## IT

### I fatti della giornata
1. ✅ **Guterres propone un "patto per la giustizia climatica"** in quattro punti: uscita dai fossili con piani nazionali che tutelino lavoratori e comunità, protezione dei più vulnerabili, finanza climatica accessibile, cooperazione contro barriere commerciali e tecnologiche [1]. Chiede di tassare gli extraprofitti delle compagnie fossili: le nove maggiori hanno guadagnato 95 mld $ in un trimestre, mentre il fondo per perdite e danni ha circa 600 mln $ [1]. "Fossil fuels must go the way of the dinosaurs" [1][8].
2. ✅ **Anche con gli NDC attuati, oltre 2,5 °C** a fine secolo, ricorda Guterres [1]. 🟡 Emissioni 2025 a livelli record e ultimi quattro anni i più caldi [7][8].
3. ✅ **Erdoğan:** tre decenni di negoziati non hanno dato risultati sufficienti; più sostegno ai Paesi in via di sviluppo [1]. ✅ La Presidenza rilancia il "Climate Implementation Bridge" e il 35-by-35, con l'1,5 °C come "bussola" [2]. ⚠️ Secondo Climate Home, né Erdoğan né Albanese hanno parlato dell'uscita dai fossili nei loro Paesi [5].
4. 🟡 **Von der Leyen sostiene il "35-by-35"** e invita altri ad aderire [7][9]. ⚠️ Climate Home scrive però che UE e Regno Unito non hanno firmato [5]: da chiarire domattina. ⚠️ Secondo Climate Home circa una dozzina di Paesi sostiene pubblicamente l'obiettivo; l'Arabia Saudita no [5].
5. ✅ **Lula annuncia roadmap nazionali** per ridurre la dipendenza dai fossili e fermare la deforestazione [1]. ⚠️ Secondo Reuters, Han Zheng (Cina) chiede che i Paesi sviluppati guidino i tagli e rispettino gli impegni finanziari [7]; Maldive ed Etiopia chiedono finanza prevedibile e tagli più profondi a chi ha più responsabilità [7].
6. ✅ **Metano:** Guterres chiede all'UNEP uno standard globale "quasi zero" per il metano dell'oil & gas; solo una su otto delle oltre 5.000 allerte satellitari UNEP dal 2023 ha avuto risposta [1]. ✅ La tavola rotonda Zero Waste proponeva di dimezzare l'aumento previsto dei rifiuti urbani e portare l'uso circolare dei materiali almeno al 15% entro il 2035 [4].
7. ⚠️ **Finanza climatica in calo:** secondo Carbon Brief gli impegni pubblici dei Paesi sviluppati scendono di oltre il 70%, con un solo grande donatore (Regno Unito) che ha rinnovato [10]. ⚠️ Secondo AFP mancano Macron, Merz, Meloni, Carney e Takaichi [8].

## EN

### Facts of the day
1. ✅ **Guterres proposes a "climate justice compact"** with four pillars: transition away from fossil fuels through national plans that protect workers and communities, protection of the most vulnerable, affordable climate finance, cooperation against trade and technology barriers [1]. He calls for taxing fossil companies' windfall profits: the nine largest earned USD 95 bn in one quarter, while the loss and damage fund holds about USD 600 m [1]. "Fossil fuels must go the way of the dinosaurs" [1][8].
2. ✅ **Even with NDCs implemented, over 2.5 °C** by the end of the century, Guterres recalls [1]. 🟡 2025 emissions at record highs and the last four years the hottest [7][8].
3. ✅ **Erdoğan:** three decades of negotiations have not delivered enough; more support for developing countries [1]. ✅ The Presidency promotes the "Climate Implementation Bridge" and 35-by-35, with 1.5 °C as the "compass" [2]. ⚠️ According to Climate Home, neither Erdoğan nor Albanese spoke about a fossil fuel exit at home [5].
4. 🟡 **Von der Leyen backs "35-by-35"** and invites others to join [7][9]. ⚠️ Climate Home, however, writes that the EU and UK have not signed up [5]: to be clarified tomorrow morning. ⚠️ According to Climate Home about a dozen countries publicly back the goal; Saudi Arabia does not [5].
5. ✅ **Lula announces national roadmaps** to reduce fossil fuel dependence and end deforestation [1]. ⚠️ According to Reuters, Han Zheng (China) says developed countries should lead cuts and honour finance commitments [7]; the Maldives and Ethiopia call for predictable finance and deeper cuts by those most responsible [7].
6. ✅ **Methane:** Guterres asks UNEP for a global "near-zero" methane standard for oil and gas; only one in eight of UNEP's 5,000+ satellite alerts since 2023 got a response [1]. ✅ The Zero Waste roundtable proposed halving the projected increase in municipal waste and raising circular material use to at least 15% by 2035 [4].
7. ⚠️ **Climate finance falling:** according to Carbon Brief, developed countries' public pledges drop by more than 70%, with only one major donor (the UK) renewing [10]. ⚠️ According to AFP, Macron, Merz, Meloni, Carney and Takaichi are absent [8].

### State of negotiations by theme

- **Mitigation / NDCs:** ✅ dispute over the duration and modalities of the emissions-acceleration programme [1]; 🟡 parties are deciding whether to continue the mitigation work programme [1][11]. ⚠️ According to Climate Home there is a fight over the timing of the IPCC's 7th assessment: Europe, small islands and LDCs want it before the 2028 Global Stocktake, India, Saudi Arabia and Russia later [5].
- **Finance:** ✅ dispute over the predictability of climate finance [1]. ⚠️ Public pledges −70% according to Carbon Brief [10]. ⚠️ TFFF at about USD 7.9 bn of 10, no new government pledges [5].
- **Article 6 (carbon markets):** no news in the day's sources.
- **Adaptation / GGA:** ✅ Guterres puts early warning and adaptation in his compact [1]. ⚠️ According to Carbon Brief's tracker, informal consultations on GGA indicators continue [11]. Baku High-Level Dialogue (10 Nov) summary not yet found.
- **Loss & damage:** ✅ open dispute, including Nepal's request after a glacier collapse [1].
- **Just transition:** ✅ dispute over the proposed mechanism [1]; 🟡 details of the Belém-Antalya Mechanism are being negotiated [1][11].
- **GST follow-up:** ⚠️ Lula wants the fossil fuel, deforestation and finance roadmaps in the next GST, according to Climate Home [5].
- **Trade / unilateral measures:** ✅ the fourth pillar of Guterres's compact mentions trade barriers [1]; nothing new on the agenda.
- **Other:** 🟡 Pezeshkian (Iran) calls for documenting the environmental damage of wars and for compensation mechanisms [7][8].
