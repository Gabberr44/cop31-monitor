# COP31 — Day 9 · Wednesday 18 November 2026
🕘 Provisional evening version — will be consolidated tomorrow morning at 09:30 (Italian time)<br>
First evening draft: 18/11/2026 at 22:50 (Italian time).

Theme of the day chosen by the **COP31 Presidency (Türkiye)**: "Human, Social Development and Gender" ✅ [1]. The high-level segment closed with the last ministers' statements ✅ [9].

## At a glance
- **What happened:** **Murat Kurum, COP31 President**, gathered ministers in a "Divan", a closed-door political exchange on the draft deal ✅ [3][6]. **Simon Stiell, UNFCCC Executive Secretary**, launched the second Global Stocktake (the collective review of progress under the Paris Agreement) ✅ [3][6].
- **Why it matters:** 21 issues on adaptation, finance, mitigation and just transition remain open ✅ [6]; the Global Stocktake will shape the next NDCs (national emission-reduction plans), due in 2030 🟡 [3].
- **Tomorrow:** new drafts of the final decision and of the technical texts are expected on Thursday morning 🟡 [4][6].

## Main facts
1. **Murat Kurum, COP31 President and Turkish Environment Minister**, chaired a "Divan", a closed-door political exchange among ministers, in the late afternoon ✅ [3][6]. According to Climate Home it started at 17:00 Antalya (15:00 Italian time) ⚠️ [3]. Ministers were asked to engage with each other rather than read statements ✅ [3][6]. After the meeting Kurum spoke of "significant gains" ⚠️ according to AFP [4].
2. **The COP31 Presidency**, in a mid-week letter, still counts 21 unresolved issues ✅ [6]. New drafts are expected on Thursday morning, with the close scheduled for Friday 🟡 [4][6]. According to **UN News (UN)**, observers expect at least one extra night ✅ [6].
3. **Simon Stiell, UNFCCC Executive Secretary**, launched the second Global Stocktake, which concludes at COP33 in 2028 ✅ [3][6][8]. He said "an ambitious outcome is within reach" despite strong geopolitical headwinds ✅ [3][6].
4. **Saudi Arabia and India**, with other large developing countries, want the next IPCC (UN climate science panel) reports postponed to 2029 ✅ [3][4][6]. **Least Developed Countries (LDCs), small island states (AOSIS) and European countries** want them before 2028 🟡 [3][4]. **Samira Nawa, Danish Climate Minister**, said: "There is no compromise when it comes to science" ⚠️ according to AFP [4].
5. **Türkiye's "35x35" goal** (electricity at 35% of final energy use by 2035) has 75 backing countries ✅ [6]. **Haitham Al Ghais, OPEC (Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries) Secretary General**, argues that more electricity will still require oil ⚠️ according to AFP [5].
6. **Inger Andersen, Executive Director of UNEP (UN Environment Programme)**, told the Group of Negative Emitters (GONE) that CO₂ removal "cannot be used to avoid transitioning away from fossil fuels" ⚠️ according to Climate Home [3]. **The first draft of the final decision** contains a goal to scale up removals; **the European Union** opposes that wording ⚠️ according to Climate Home [7].
7. **Portugal and Ireland** presented national roadmaps to phase out coal, oil and gas ⚠️ according to AFP [4]. **Juan Carlos Navarro, Panama's Environment Minister**, said COPs are "doomed to failure" as long as they decide by consensus ⚠️ according to Climate Home [3].

🎥 Videos: no official recording of the Global Stocktake launch or of the last ministers' statements found on UN Web TV or UNFCCC channels at the time of the evening draft. The Divan was closed-door.
