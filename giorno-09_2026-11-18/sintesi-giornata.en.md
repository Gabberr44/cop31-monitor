# COP31 — Day 9 · Wednesday 18 November 2026
✅ Final version — consolidated on 19/11/2026 at 10:05 (Italian time)<br>
First evening draft: 18/11/2026 at 22:50 (Italian time).

Theme of the day chosen by the **COP31 Presidency (Türkiye)**: "Human, Social Development and Gender" ✅ [1]. The high-level segment closed with the last ministers' statements ✅ [9].

## At a glance
- **What happened:** **Murat Kurum, COP31 President**, gathered ministers in a "Divan", a closed-door political exchange on the draft deal ✅ [3][6]. The exchange produced no clear breakthrough 🟡 [11][12]. **Simon Stiell, UNFCCC Executive Secretary**, launched the second Global Stocktake (the collective review of progress under the Paris Agreement) ✅ [3][6].
- **Why it matters:** 21 issues on adaptation, finance, mitigation and just transition remain open ✅ [6]; the Global Stocktake will shape the next NDCs (national emission-reduction plans), due in 2030 🟡 [3].
- **Tomorrow:** a new, slimmer draft of the final decision is expected on Thursday morning 🟡 [11][12]; the first part of the closing plenaries is scheduled for the afternoon ✅ [13].

## Main facts
1. **Murat Kurum, COP31 President and Turkish Environment Minister**, chaired a "Divan", a closed-door political exchange among ministers, in the late afternoon ✅ [3][6]. Ministers were asked to engage with each other rather than read statements ✅ [3][6]. Kurum spoke of "significant progress" ⚠️ according to AFP [4][12].<br>
   **Climate Home News** reports instead that the Divan made little concrete progress; **French minister Monique Barbut** spoke of "a lot of listening", without saying how to break the deadlock 🟡 [11][12].
2. **Adão Soares Barbosa, Timor-Leste's climate ambassador and chair of the Least Developed Countries (LDC) group**, called the Divan "constructive" ⚠️ according to Climate Home [11]. **Darragh O'Brien, Irish Environment Minister for the rotating EU Presidency**, asked for the next text to be "slimmer and focused" ⚠️ according to AFP [12].
3. **The COP31 Presidency**, in a mid-week letter, still counts 21 unresolved issues ✅ [6]. New drafts are expected on Thursday morning, with the close scheduled for Friday 🟡 [4][6]. According to **UN News (UN)**, observers expect at least one extra night ✅ [6].
4. **Simon Stiell, UNFCCC Executive Secretary**, launched the second Global Stocktake, which concludes at COP33 in 2028 ✅ [3][6][8]. He said "an ambitious outcome is within reach" despite strong geopolitical headwinds ✅ [3][6].
5. **Saudi Arabia and India**, with other large developing countries, want the next IPCC (UN climate science panel) reports postponed to 2029 ✅ [3][4][6]. **Least Developed Countries (LDCs), small island states (AOSIS) and European countries** want them before 2028 🟡 [3][4]. **Samira Nawa, Danish Climate Minister**, said: "There is no compromise when it comes to science" ⚠️ according to AFP [4][12].
6. **Türkiye's "35x35" goal** (electricity at 35% of final energy use by 2035) has 75 backing countries ✅ [6]. **Haitham Al Ghais, OPEC (Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries) Secretary General**, argues that more electricity will still require oil ⚠️ according to AFP [5].
7. **Inger Andersen, Executive Director of UNEP (UN Environment Programme)**, told the Group of Negative Emitters (GONE) that CO₂ removal "cannot be used to avoid transitioning away from fossil fuels" ⚠️ according to Climate Home [3]. **The first draft of the final decision** contains a goal to scale up removals; **the European Union** opposes that wording ⚠️ according to Climate Home [7].
8. **Portugal and Ireland** presented national roadmaps to phase out coal, oil and gas ⚠️ according to AFP [4][12]. **Maina Talia, Tuvalu's minister**, said phasing out fossil fuels is "a question of survival for all of us" ⚠️ according to AFP [12].
9. **Kim Sung-whan, South Korea's Energy and Environment Minister**, said South Korea is "seriously" considering hosting COP33 in 2028 ⚠️ according to AFP [12]. **Juan Carlos Navarro, Panama's Environment Minister**, said COPs are "doomed to failure" as long as they decide by consensus ⚠️ according to Climate Home [3].

🎥 Videos: no official recording of the Global Stocktake launch or of the last ministers' statements found on UN Web TV or UNFCCC channels (re-checked on the morning of 19/11). The Divan was closed-door.
