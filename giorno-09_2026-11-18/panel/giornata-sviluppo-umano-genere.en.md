# Human, social development and gender day
**When:** 18/11, time n/a · **Where:** Antalya EXPO Center · **Organiser:** COP31 Presidency (thematic day); high-level event on engaging women, girls, men and boys<br>
**Speakers:** **Lilian Chagas** (Brazilian climate negotiator); **Feleti Teo** (Prime Minister of Tuvalu); **Shen Xiaomeng** (Vice-Rector of the United Nations University, UNU)<br>
🎥 Video not yet available

## At a glance
The Presidency's thematic day discussed women's invisible work and the future of jobs. The topic links to the just transition, one of the files still open in the negotiations.

## Key points
- **Lilian Chagas, Brazilian climate negotiator**, said excluding women from climate planning makes action less fair and less effective ✅ [1].
- **Feleti Teo, Prime Minister of Tuvalu**, described women on Funafuti monitoring dwindling shellfish, rationing rainwater and moving crops inland ✅ [1].
- **Shen Xiaomeng, UNU Vice-Rector and UNU-EHS Director**, said development should aim at human well-being ✅ [1].
- **Mariana Mazzucato, economist**, said the just transition is too often reduced to finding more money ✅ according to UN News [1].

## Relevance for the VSME
**Low** — No effect on climate reporting. The topic does, however, echo the VSME's social workforce indicators (B8–B10).
