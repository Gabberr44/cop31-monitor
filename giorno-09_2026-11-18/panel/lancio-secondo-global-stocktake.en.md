# Launch of the second Global Stocktake
**When:** 18/11, 15:00–18:00 Antalya (13:00–16:00 Italian time), according to the UNFCCC page · **Where:** Antalya EXPO Center · **Organiser:** COP31 Presidency with the UNFCCC secretariat<br>
**Speakers:** **Simon Stiell** (UNFCCC Executive Secretary); **Jim Skea** (IPCC Chair); ministers<br>
🎥 Video not yet available

## At a glance
The second Global Stocktake (GST) has started: the five-yearly review that checks whether countries' plans are enough to meet the Paris Agreement. The first, concluded at COP28 in Dubai, led to the goals of tripling renewables and transitioning away from fossil fuels.

## Key points
- **Simon Stiell, UNFCCC Executive Secretary**, called the launch "a major moment" and "a major opportunity" 🟡 according to Climate Home [1].
- **Simon Stiell** said "an ambitious outcome is within reach" despite strong geopolitical headwinds ✅ [1][2].
- **The process** lasts two years and concludes at COP33 in 2028, the last review before 2030 ✅ [1][2]. According to the UNFCCC page it starts at CMA8 (this year's meeting of the Parties to the Paris Agreement) and ends at CMA10 ⚠️ [3].
- **Jim Skea, Chair of the IPCC (UN climate science panel)**, said the Working Group II report will cover adaptation finance for the first time ✅ [2].
- **Saudi Arabia, India and other large developing countries** want IPCC reports postponed to 2029; **European countries and small island states** oppose ✅ [1][2]. The IPCC will revisit the issue in early December 🟡 [1].

## Announcements and numbers
- **The second Global Stocktake** will inform the next round of NDCs (national emission-reduction plans), due in 2030 🟡 according to Climate Home [1].

## Quotes
- "We have two years." — **Simon Stiell, UNFCCC Executive Secretary** ✅ [2]

## Relevance for the VSME
**Medium** — No immediate obligation. The GST will push for more ambitious NDCs from 2030, which then become national and EU laws: SMEs already measuring emissions (B3) will be ready.
