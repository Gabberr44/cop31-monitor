# Overtime: the Presidency's consultations
**When:** 21/11, all day, until the night plenary between Saturday and Sunday (times n.a.) · **Where:** Antalya EXPO Center, negotiating rooms · **Organiser:** COP31 Presidencies (Türkiye and Australia)<br>
**Speakers:** **Murat Kurum** (COP31 President, Turkish Environment Minister); **Wopke Hoekstra** (EU Climate Commissioner); **Isatou Camara** (finance negotiator, Gambia); **Brenda Mwale** (loss and damage negotiator, Malawi)<br>
🎥 No video expected for the consultations (closed doors); night plenary: video not yet available

## In brief
All Saturday **the COP31 Presidency** negotiated in private; the third draft of the final decision only arrived overnight 🟡 [1][2][4]. It matters because the fossil-fuel compromise is being decided now, while many delegates are starting to leave ⚠️ [2].

## Key points
- **Murat Kurum, COP31 President**, met representatives of the European Union, Australia and Saudi Arabia ⚠️ according to Reuters [1].
- **The COP31 Presidency** consulted the negotiating groups one by one on sections of the new text ⚠️ according to Carbon Brief [2].
- **The heads-of-delegation meeting** at 16:00 Antalya (14:00 Italian time) was cancelled ⚠️ [2].
- **The G77+China** (the developing-country group) and **the European Union** held separate coordination meetings ⚠️ [2].
- **The draft under discussion** acknowledges that the world will overshoot 1.5 °C and calls for more CO₂-removal technologies; some delegations fear it would justify more fossil fuels ⚠️ according to Reuters [1].
- **Hürriyet Daily News** reports that negotiating teams worked overnight on the third draft, expected on 21/11 ⚠️ [3].

- **Murat Kurum**, at a night plenary, said he had put forward "a text which all the parties can agree upon" and convened the approval for Sunday morning ⚠️ according to Reuters [4]. He asked Parties to "come to us with agreement on the text" by 06:00 Antalya ⚠️ according to Carbon Brief [5].
- **The COP31 Presidency** published the third draft at 03:00 Antalya on Sunday (01:00 Italian time) 🟡 [4][5]. Details in the panel [The third draft of the final decision](terza-bozza-decisione-finale.en.md).
## Quotes
- "It's either going to be a good deal or it's going to be no deal." — **Wopke Hoekstra, EU Climate Commissioner** [1]
- "We believe that we have put forward a text which all the parties can agree upon." — **Murat Kurum, COP31 President** [4]

## Relevance for the VSME
**Medium** — whether or not the final decision mentions the transition away from fossil fuels will steer national energy policies, and therefore the energy and emissions data that banks and customers request from SMEs (VSME B3).
