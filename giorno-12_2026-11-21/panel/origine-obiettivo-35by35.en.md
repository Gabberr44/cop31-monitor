# Where "35 by 35" comes from
**When:** 21/11 (explainer) · **Where:** Carbon Brief Connect (online forum) · **Organiser:** Carbon Brief<br>
**Speakers:** **Josh Gabbatiss** (Carbon Brief); **Simon Evans** (Carbon Brief)<br>
🎥 Not applicable (written text)

## In brief
The goal of raising electricity to 35% of final energy consumption by 2035 comes from IRENA and IEA analysis ⚠️ according to Carbon Brief [1]. It matters because it is the outcome the Presidency is pushing hardest and the one with the most voluntary endorsements ⚠️ [1][2].

## Key points
- **Carbon Brief** estimates that electricity covers around 23% of global final energy consumption today ⚠️ [1].
- **IRENA (the International Renewable Energy Agency)** indicates, in a May 2026 report, 35% by 2035 and more than 50% by 2050 to stay on a 1.5 °C-compatible pathway ⚠️ according to Carbon Brief [1].
- **IRENA's estimates** differ by region: 42% by 2035 in the European Union, 26% in Africa ⚠️ [1].
- **Simon Evans, of Carbon Brief**, recalls that as early as September 2024 the **IEA (the International Energy Agency)** listed 35% by 2035 as a benchmark consistent with the COP28 outcome ⚠️ [1].
- **Murat Kurum, then COP31 President-designate**, launched the goal at the June 2026 Bonn session as a flagship initiative of the Action Agenda ⚠️ [1].
- **Hürriyet Daily News** reports that 75 countries and 94 institutions and organisations back it ⚠️ reported by a single source [2].
