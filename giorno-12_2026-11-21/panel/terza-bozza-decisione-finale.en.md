# The third draft of the final decision
**When:** 22/11, 03:00 Antalya (01:00 Italian time), during the night of Day 12 · **Where:** Antalya EXPO Center; text released by the Presidency · **Organiser:** COP31 Presidencies (Türkiye and Australia)<br>
**Speakers:** **Murat Kurum** (COP31 President, Turkish Environment Minister)<br>
🎥 Video not yet available (night presentation plenary)

## In brief
**The COP31 Presidency** published overnight the third version of the "cover decision" (the political decision that closes the COP) 🟡 [1][2]. It matters because it is the text Sunday's plenary must approve by consensus, that is with no objections 🟡 [1][2].

## Key points
- **The text** does not explicitly mention fossil fuels 🟡 [1][2]. It recalls "in its entirety" the outcome of the first Global Stocktake (COP28's global review), which referred to transitioning away from fossil fuels ⚠️ according to Carbon Brief [2].
- **The text** acknowledges that warming is on track to exceed 1.5 °C 🟡 [1][2]. According to Carbon Brief it sets up a "high-level dialogue" on 1.5 °C science at COP32 ⚠️ [2].
- **The text** calls for faster carbon capture and removal 🟡 [1][2]. It drops figures for CCUS (carbon capture, utilisation and storage) but cites an aspirational "gigatonne scale" by 2035 ⚠️ [2].
- **On adaptation finance** there is no USD 120 billion figure: the text "agrees to discuss" a pathway to triple it ⚠️ according to Carbon Brief [2].
- **On trade** the text "notes" that trade measures may have cross-border impacts, particularly for developing countries ⚠️ [2].
- **The text** acknowledges that the global transition to low emissions is "irreversible" and keeps the "35 by 35" goal in a weak paragraph ⚠️ [2].

## Announcements and numbers
- 94 paragraphs, vs 144 in the first draft (16/11) and 80 in the second (19/11) ⚠️ [2].
- Approval plenary convened for 09:00 Antalya (07:00 Italian time) on Sunday 22/11 🟡 [1][2].

## Quotes
- "We believe that we have put forward a text which all the parties can agree upon." — **Murat Kurum, COP31 President** [1]

## Relevance for the VSME
**Medium** — no new obligation for SMEs, but acknowledging the 1.5 °C overshoot strengthens bank and customer questions on physical climate risks; the push for removals does not change the rule of reporting gross emissions.
