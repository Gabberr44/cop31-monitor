# COP31 — Day 12 · Saturday 21 November 2026
🕘 Provisional evening version — will be consolidated tomorrow morning at 09:30 (Italian time)<br>
⏱️ Second day of overtime: at 22:40 Italian time (23:40 Antalya) no third draft of the final decision had been published and no closing plenary had been convened 🟡 [1][2].

A day not in the official calendar: COP31 was due to close on Friday 20/11 ✅ [7]. Public events and exhibitions have ended; only the negotiating rooms remain open ⚠️ [4].

## In brief
- **What happened:** **the COP31 Presidency** consulted the negotiating groups one at a time, but published no new draft all day 🟡 [1][2].
- **Why it matters:** the longer it lasts, the more delegates leave; without a minimum number of countries present (the "quorum") the COP cannot decide and would be suspended ⚠️ [2].
- **Tomorrow:** on Sunday 22/11 the third draft and the closing plenary are expected, with no confirmed time ⚠️ [1][4].

## Key facts
1. **The COP31 Presidency** had still not published a new version of the final decision (the "cover decision") on Saturday afternoon 🟡 [1][2]. Thursday 19/11's text remains on the table: it does not mention fossil fuels and no longer contains the USD 120 billion figure for adaptation ✅ [6] 🟡 [1].<br>
   🎥 No video expected (closed-door consultations)
2. **Murat Kurum, COP31 President and Turkish Environment Minister**, met representatives of the European Union, Australia and Saudi Arabia to break the deadlock ⚠️ according to Reuters [1]. He said there is "no benefit for anyone" in ruining the process "by getting hung up on words, phrases" ⚠️ [1].
3. **The COP31 Presidency** consulted the negotiating groups one by one on sections of the new text ⚠️ according to Carbon Brief [2]. **The heads-of-delegation meeting** scheduled at 16:00 Antalya (14:00 Italian time) was cancelled ⚠️ [2]. **The G77+China (the group of over 130 developing countries)** and **the European Union** held separate coordination meetings ⚠️ [2].
4. **Wopke Hoekstra, EU Climate Commissioner**, said: "It's either going to be a good deal or it's going to be no deal" ⚠️ according to Reuters [1]. According to **Carbon Brief**, more than 130 countries want the transition away from fossil fuels restored to the text ⚠️ [2].
5. **Isatou Camara, Gambia's finance negotiator**, accuses some countries of using delay tactics to force a weaker outcome ⚠️ according to Reuters [1]. **Brenda Mwale, Malawi's loss and damage negotiator**, speaks of "a big problem" given the amount of unfinished business ⚠️ [1].
6. **Some delegations** left the venue for the night ⚠️ according to Carbon Brief [2]. **Lynda Tabuya, Fiji's Climate Minister**, moved her return flight to Monday ⚠️ according to Carbon Brief, citing the Guardian [2].
7. **The "35 by 35" electrification goal** (35% electricity in final energy consumption by 2035) comes from a May 2026 report by **IRENA (the International Renewable Energy Agency)** ⚠️ according to Carbon Brief [3]. Today the global share is around 23% ⚠️ [3]. According to **Hürriyet Daily News**, 75 countries and 94 institutions back it ⚠️ reported by a single source [4].
