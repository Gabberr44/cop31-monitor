# COP31 — Day 12 · Saturday 21 November 2026
✅ Final version — consolidated on 22/11/2026 at 09:50 (Italian time)<br>
⏱️ Second day of overtime. No new draft all day; the third draft of the final decision came out overnight, at 03:00 Antalya on Sunday (01:00 Italian time) 🟡 [8][9].

A day not in the official calendar: COP31 was due to close on Friday 20/11 ✅ [7]. Public events and exhibitions were over; only the negotiating rooms stayed open ⚠️ [4].

## In brief
- **What happened:** **the COP31 Presidency** consulted the negotiating groups all Saturday and only published the third draft of the final decision overnight 🟡 [2][8][9].
- **Why it matters:** the new text does not name fossil fuels, but recalls the COP28 agreement on the transition; it is the basis for Sunday's approval 🟡 [8][9].
- **Tomorrow:** on Sunday 22/11 at 09:00 Antalya (07:00 Italian time) the closing plenary is convened to approve the package 🟡 [8][9].

## Key facts
1. **The COP31 Presidency** published no new texts all Saturday 🟡 [1][2]. Until night, the table held Thursday 19/11's draft, without the USD 120 billion adaptation figure ✅ [6].<br>
   🎥 No video expected (closed-door consultations)
2. **Murat Kurum, COP31 President and Turkish Environment Minister**, met representatives of the European Union, Australia and Saudi Arabia ⚠️ according to Reuters [1]. He said "no one has anything to gain" from derailing the process "by fixating on words and phrases" ⚠️ [1].
3. **The heads-of-delegation meeting** at 16:00 Antalya (14:00 Italian time) was cancelled ⚠️ according to Carbon Brief [2]. **The G77+China (the group of over 130 developing countries)** and **the European Union** met separately ⚠️ [2].
4. **Wopke Hoekstra, European Commissioner for Climate**, said: "It will either be a good deal, or there will be no deal" ⚠️ according to Reuters [1]. **Isatou Camara, finance negotiator for The Gambia**, accused some countries of delaying tactics ⚠️ [1].
5. **Murat Kurum**, at a plenary during the night between Saturday and Sunday, said: "We believe that we have put forward a text which all the parties can agree upon" ⚠️ according to Reuters [8]. In Kurum's view, concerns on fossil fuels and adaptation finance have been addressed ⚠️ [8].<br>
   🎥 Video not yet available
6. **The COP31 Presidency** published the third draft of the final decision at 03:00 Antalya on Sunday (01:00 Italian time) 🟡 [8][9]. The text does not explicitly mention fossil fuels, but recalls an earlier COP agreement on transitioning away from oil, gas and coal 🟡 [8][9]. It acknowledges that warming is on track to exceed 1.5 °C and calls for faster carbon-removal and capture technologies 🟡 [8][9].
7. **Carbon Brief** reports that texts on just transition, the mitigation work programme, the Adaptation Fund and finance also came out overnight ⚠️ [10]. The just transition mechanism would be designed in Bonn in 2027 and adopted at COP32 ⚠️ [10]. The global goal on adaptation and national adaptation plans still have no text ⚠️ [10].

## Negotiations: where we stand
| Topic | What moved | What is blocked | Texts released |
|---|---|---|---|
| Final decision ("cover decision") | Third draft overnight 🟡 [8][9]; 94 paragraphs, vs 144 in the first and 80 in the second ⚠️ [9] | Fossil-fuel wording: only an indirect reference to the COP28 agreement 🟡 [8][9] | Third draft, 22/11 at 03:00 Antalya 🟡 [8][9] |
| NDCs (national emission-reduction plans) / mitigation | "35 by 35" goal (35% electricity in final energy use by 2035) kept, but in a weak paragraph ⚠️ [9] | Carbon removal and capture pushed "at gigatonne scale" by 2035, without binding figures ⚠️ [9] | Mitigation work programme, little changed ⚠️ [10] |
| Finance and adaptation / GGA (the global goal on adaptation) | Proposal to "discuss a pathway" to triple adaptation finance ⚠️ [9] | No USD 120 billion figure ⚠️ [9]; no GGA text ⚠️ [10] | Finance texts without square brackets ⚠️ [10] |
| Loss and damage | The text calls for continued efforts to scale up the fund's resources ⚠️ [9] | Resources described as "insufficient" versus demand ⚠️ [10] | Revised fund report ⚠️ [10] |
| Just transition | Phased approach: functions in Bonn 2027, adoption at COP32 ⚠️ [10] | Mechanism postponed by a year ⚠️ [10] | First draft decision ⚠️ [10] |
| Trade / unilateral measures | The draft "notes" that trade measures may have cross-border impacts ⚠️ [9] | New impact assessment of cross-border measures removed ⚠️ [10] | "Response measures" text ⚠️ [10] |
