# The second draft of the final decision
**When:** 19/11, published mid-afternoon; version dated 14:10 Antalya (12:10 Italian time) · **Where:** UNFCCC website · **Organiser:** COP31 Presidency (Türkiye) with the Presidency of Negotiations (Australia)<br>
**Speakers:** **Murat Kurum** (COP31 President, Turkish Environment Minister); **Chris Bowen** (President of Negotiations, Australian Climate Minister)<br>
🎥 Video not applicable (written text)

## In brief
The second draft of the "cover decision", the political text that closes the COP, halves the first version and removes every reference to fossil fuels ✅ [1]. It matters because it sets the basis on which COP31 will close: yes to electrification, no to a fossil-fuel exit, adaptation finance postponed.

## Key points
- **The COP31 Presidency** cut the text from 144 to 80 paragraphs, on 9 pages ✅ [1][2]. The words "fossil fuels", oil, gas and coal no longer appear ✅ [1].
- **The draft** refers to an "irreversible global transition towards low greenhouse gas emissions and climate resilience" ✅ [1]. **Chris Bowen** defended this wording ⚠️ according to Climate Home [5].
- **The draft** calls for raising electricity to 35% of final energy consumption by 2035, from about 23% today ✅ [1] 🟡 [3][6].
- **The draft** proposes accelerating CCUS (carbon capture, utilisation and storage), with a bracketed target of at least 500 million tonnes a year by 2035 ✅ [1]. **The European Union** opposed this reference ⚠️ according to Il Sole 24 Ore [7].
- **The draft** drops the USD 120 billion adaptation figure 🟡 [3][4]. It asks the Standing Committee on Finance for a report on the baseline for tripling adaptation finance by 2035 ✅ [1].
- **The draft** "notes the concerns" about trade measures with cross-border effects, such as CBAM (the EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism) ✅ [1]. The first version expressed "serious concerns" 🟡 [2].

## Announcements and figures
- **167 Parties** had submitted new NDCs (national emission-reduction plans) as at 18/11/2026 ✅ [1].
- **140 Parties** have submitted their first biennial transparency report; the next ones are due by 31/12/2026 ✅ [1].
- **Developing-country needs:** USD 455–584 billion a year up to 2030; USD 305 billion a year for adaptation up to 2035 ✅ [1].
- **Antalya-to-Addis Ababa Roadmap** on adaptation and resilience finance, reporting to CMA 9 in 2027 ✅ [1].

## Quotes
- "This new text has gone backwards from the one released earlier in the week." — **Mohamed Adow, director of Power Shift Africa** 🟡 [3][4]

## Relevance for the VSME
**Medium** — no new obligation for SMEs, but the draft confirms the direction: more electrification, "high-integrity" carbon markets and no reversal on CBAM.
