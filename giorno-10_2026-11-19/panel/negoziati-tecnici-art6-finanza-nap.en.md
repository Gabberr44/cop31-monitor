# Technical negotiations: Art. 6, finance, adaptation plans
**When:** 19/11, all day and evening · **Where:** informal consultation rooms, Antalya EXPO Center · **Organiser:** COP and CMA (the meeting of the Paris Agreement Parties) bodies, with co-facilitators appointed by Parties<br>
**Speakers:** **Peer Stiansen** (co-facilitator, Norway); **Nikita Charles Hamilton** (The Bahamas) and **Angela Friedrich** (Austria); **Audrey Bernard** (Canada) and **Thomas Lerenten Lelekoitien** (Kenya); **Noura Alissa** (Saudi Arabia); **Amena Yauvoli** (Fiji)<br>
🎥 Video not applicable (closed informal consultations)

## In brief
On the last full day of technical work many items are closing with procedural decisions only, i.e. postponed to future sessions ⚠️ [1]. It matters because technical details, such as carbon-market rules, slip to 2027.

## Key points
- **Peer Stiansen, co-facilitator on Art. 6.2 (country-to-country credit trading)**, proposed a "light-touch" revision of the text on international registry fees, to be resumed in 2027 ⚠️ according to ENB [1].
- **Many Parties** asked the secretariat for a note on fee levels, costs and exemptions; **the EU** asked for timelines to make the system self-financing ⚠️ [1]. **AILAC (Independent Alliance of Latin America and the Caribbean)** urged no delay to the registry ⚠️ [1].
- **Parties** did not agree on non-market approaches (Art. 6.8): the text goes to SB66 (June 2027) ⚠️ according to ENB [1].
- **Parties** did not agree on national adaptation plans (NAPs): the informal note goes to SB66 ⚠️ according to ENB [1].
- **Noura Alissa, co-facilitator on the annual Global Stocktake dialogue**, proposed a text to be accepted without changes; **Norway** rejected wording that weakens the link between the Global Stocktake and NDCs ⚠️ [1]. A procedural decision is likely ⚠️ [1].
- **On the Standing Committee on Finance**, texts are almost "clean" and go to the Presidency ⚠️ according to ENB [1].

## Relevance for the VSME
**Medium** — the postponement of Art. 6 rules leaves carbon-credit conditions open: SMEs buying credits must keep reporting them separately from B3 emissions.
