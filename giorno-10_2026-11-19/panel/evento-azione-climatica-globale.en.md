# High-level event on Global Climate Action
**When:** 19/11, 14:00–16:00 Antalya (12:00–14:00 Italian time) · **Where:** Plenary Hall 2, Antalya EXPO Center · **Organiser:** COP30 (Brazil) and COP31 (Türkiye and Australia) Presidencies, UNFCCC<br>
**Speakers:** **Fatma Varank** (COP31 Chief Executive); **Simon Stiell** (UNFCCC Executive Secretary); **Ana Toni** (COP30 Chief Executive); **David Higgins** (COP31 Presidency of Negotiations); **Rikki Dank** (Gudanji For Country)<br>
🎥 Video not yet available (re-checked on the morning of 20/11)

## In brief
This is the official event mandated by COP decisions to take stock of the Action Agenda: voluntary initiatives by businesses, cities and coalitions, outside the negotiations ✅ [2] ⚠️ [1]. It matters because voluntary goals such as "35x35" start here and then enter the negotiating texts.

## Key points
- **Fatma Varank, COP31 Chief Executive**, presented Türkiye's six voluntary global goals, including the package on 35% electrification by 2035 ⚠️ according to ENB [1].
- **Simon Stiell, UNFCCC Executive Secretary**, invited countries to nominate voluntary national focal points for the Action Agenda ⚠️ according to ENB [1].
- **Ana Toni, COP30 Chief Executive**, stressed the importance of transparency to measure progress of voluntary initiatives ⚠️ according to ENB [1].
- **David Higgins, of the COP31 Presidency of Negotiations**, presented an Australia–Republic of Korea memorandum to unlock USD 1 trillion for decarbonisation ⚠️ according to ENB [1].
- **The European Union** asked to link voluntary initiatives to the mitigation work programme; **AOSIS (the Alliance of Small Island States)** asked for the same level of investment in adaptation as in mitigation ⚠️ [1].
- **Switzerland** announced the publication of its own roadmap for transitioning away from fossil fuels ⚠️ according to ENB [1].

## Quotes
- The numbers in the 2026 Yearbook of Global Climate Action "are not the measures we live by". — **Alliance of Small Island States (AOSIS)** ⚠️ [1]

## Relevance for the VSME
**Medium** — voluntary initiatives increasingly ask for transparency on progress: companies joining coalitions will need to measure and communicate data consistent with the VSME.
