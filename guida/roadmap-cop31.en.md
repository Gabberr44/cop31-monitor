## After the COP
| Date | Milestone | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 19/03/2027 | **EU member states** must transpose the Omnibus I directive (new CSRD scope and protection of SMEs in the value chain) ✅ | [13] |
| Financial years from 01/01/2027 | **CSRD "second-wave" companies** (over 1,000 employees and EUR 450 m turnover) start reporting ✅ | [13] |
| 01/02/2027 | **EU member states** start selling CBAM certificates on a common platform ✅ | [14] |
| June 2027 (dates to be confirmed) | **The subsidiary bodies (SB66)** meet in Bonn ✅; the UN greenhouse-gas data interface returns to the agenda 🟡 | [1][10] |
| 30/09/2027 | **EU importers** file the first annual CBAM declaration, for 2026 imports ✅ | [14] |
| 8–19/11/2027 | **COP32** takes place in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia ✅ | [1] |
| 6–17/11/2028 | **COP33** will be held in an Asia-Pacific country; host to be confirmed ✅. **China** has withdrawn its bid 🟡 | [1][15] |
| Date to be confirmed | **The European Commission**: acts on the voluntary reporting standard for SMEs (VSME) | — |
