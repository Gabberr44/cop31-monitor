# COP31 — Day 4 · Thursday 12 November 2026
🕘 Provisional evening version — will be consolidated tomorrow morning at 06:00 (Italian time)<br>
Updated on 12/11/2026 at 22:15 (Italian time). The IISD ENB report for the day is not yet available.

Theme of the day chosen by the **COP31 Presidency (Türkiye)**: "Resilient Cities and Built Environment" ✅ [2]. It is the second and last day of the Leaders' Summit ✅ [1].

## In brief
- **What happened:** about 25 **heads of state and government** spoke on the summit's second day ⚠️ according to Climate Home [3]. In parallel, the non-CO₂ greenhouse gases summit and the Pacific pledging event took place ✅ [1].
- **Why it matters:** **Russia, Saudi Arabia and the LMDC group (Like-Minded Developing Countries, including China and India)** question whether a political final decision is needed at all ⚠️ according to Climate Home [3].
- **Tomorrow:** the Presidency dedicates Friday 13 to "Finance and Trade" ✅ [2]. **Australia** is expected to publish a summary of initial consultations on the final decision ⚠️ [3].

## Key facts
1. **Andy Burnham, UK Prime Minister**, appointed **Dame Madeline Alessandri** as the UK's first special representative for climate security ✅ [4]. He announced backing for **Amplify**, which aims to raise £5 billion of private finance for clean energy ✅ [4].<br>
   🎥 Video not yet available
2. **Russia, Saudi Arabia and the LMDCs** have repeatedly questioned the Australian-led process for the "cover decision" (the COP's political final decision) ⚠️ according to Climate Home [3]. **Cosima Cassel, E3G analyst**, said some players "want to stall the process" ⚠️ [3].
3. **China, the United Kingdom and Brazil** co-hosted the leaders' summit on non-CO₂ greenhouse gases ✅ [1][8]. Philanthropic donors pledged more than USD 480 million to cut methane and other "super pollutants" ⚠️ according to Health Policy Watch [8].
4. **Monique Barbut, French Minister for Ecological Transition**, asked that the final decision mention methane and target "near-zero" oil and gas methane by 2030 ⚠️ according to Climate Home and Health Policy Watch [3][8]. **Mia Mottley, Prime Minister of Barbados**, called for a binding agreement instead of voluntary pledges ⚠️ [3][8].
5. **Donor countries (Netherlands, United Kingdom, Canada, Ireland)** only re-announced existing pledges at the **Pacific Resilience Facility** event (the Pacific islands' resilience fund) ⚠️ according to Climate Home [9]. The fund exceeds USD 200 million but is about USD 300 million short of its 2026 target ⚠️ [9].
6. **Matthew Wale, Prime Minister of Solomon Islands**, announced USD 400,000 for the next replenishment of the GCF (the UN Green Climate Fund) ✅ [5]. **Mafalda Duarte, GCF Executive Director**, urged large economies to follow suit ✅ [5].
7. **Rifat Hisarcıklıoğlu, President of TOBB (Union of Chambers and Commodity Exchanges of Türkiye) and COP31 Private Sector Envoy**, called for common rules to measure carbon ⚠️ [7]. He also asked that companies not have to report the same information repeatedly ⚠️ single source (Anadolu) [7].
