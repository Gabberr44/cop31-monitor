# COP31 — Day 4 · Thursday 12 November 2026
🕘 Provisional evening version — will be consolidated tomorrow morning at 06:00 (Italian time)<br>
⚠️ Day in progress — updated on 12/11/2026 at 13:00 (Italian time). Many afternoon events have no written account yet.

Theme of the day chosen by the **COP31 Presidency (Türkiye)**: "Resilient Cities and Built Environment" ✅ [2]. It is the second and last day of the Leaders' Summit ✅ [1].

## In brief
- **What happened:** **heads of state and government** delivered national statements on the second day of the summit, while the COP31 Business Forum opened ✅ [1]. According to Climate Home, about 25 leaders were on the list ⚠️ [3].
- **Why it matters:** from tomorrow only negotiators remain, and they must turn the leaders' announcements into texts ⚠️ according to Climate Home [3].
- **Tomorrow:** the Presidency dedicates Friday 13 to "Finance and Trade" ✅ [2].

## Key facts
1. **Andy Burnham, UK Prime Minister**, appointed **Dame Madeline Alessandri** as the UK's first special representative for climate security ✅ [4]. Burnham said: "Climate is a national security issue" ✅ [4].<br>
   🎥 Video not yet available
2. **Andy Burnham** announced UK backing for **Amplify**, a programme that aims to raise £5 billion of private finance for clean energy ✅ [4]. He pledged to make climate resilience a priority of next year's UK G20 presidency ✅ [4].
3. **Matthew Wale, Prime Minister of Solomon Islands**, announced USD 400,000 for the next replenishment of the GCF (the UN Green Climate Fund) ✅ [5]. It is the first pledge from a country that is both a small island developing state and a least developed country ✅ [5].
4. **Mafalda Duarte, GCF Executive Director**, urged large economies to follow Solomon Islands' lead ✅ [5]. The fund has committed USD 22.3 billion in more than 130 countries so far ✅ [5].
5. **Rifat Hisarcıklıoğlu, President of TOBB (Union of Chambers and Commodity Exchanges of Türkiye) and COP31 Private Sector Envoy**, called for common rules to measure carbon ⚠️ [7]. He also asked that companies not have to report the same information repeatedly ⚠️ single source (Anadolu) [7].
6. **Greta Thunberg, Swedish activist**, said the planet "is not dying, it is being killed" 🟡 [3][6].
7. **Monique Barbut, French Environment Minister**, called for a strong reference to methane in the COP's final decision ⚠️ according to Climate Home [3]. **Chris Bowen, Australian Climate Minister and President of Negotiations**, said Australia will sign a voluntary "near-zero" methane pledge for fossil fuels ⚠️ according to Climate Home [3].
