# COP31 — Day 4 · Thursday 12 November 2026
✅ Final version — consolidated on 13/11/2026 at 06:45 (Italian time)<br>
First evening version: 12/11/2026 at 22:15 (Italian time). The final version adds IISD ENB report no. 905 (Earth Negotiations Bulletin, the daily bulletin on the negotiations) and UN News coverage.

Theme of the day chosen by the **COP31 Presidency (Türkiye)**: "Resilient Cities and Built Environment" ✅ [2]. It is the second and last day of the Leaders' Summit ✅ [1].

## In brief
- **What happened:** about 25 **heads of state and government** closed the Leaders' Summit 🟡 [3]. In the negotiating rooms, Parties worked on technical drafts: finance, carbon markets, adaptation 🟡 [11].
- **Why it matters:** the **LMDC group (Like-Minded Developing Countries, including China and India)** asks to avoid "unnecessary cover decisions" 🟡 [3][11]. The COP's political final decision remains in doubt.
- **Tomorrow:** Friday 13 is "Finance and Trade" day, with the ministerial dialogue on climate finance ✅ [2][14]. Written inputs on the final decision are due by Saturday ✅ [12].

## Key facts
1. **Andy Burnham, UK Prime Minister**, appointed **Dame Madeline Alessandri** as the UK's first special representative for climate security ✅ [4][12]. He announced backing for **Amplify**, which aims to raise £5 billion of private finance for clean energy ✅ [4].<br>
   🎥 Video not yet available
2. **The LMDC group** asked to avoid "unnecessary cover decisions" in the Presidency consultations 🟡 [11]. **Russia and Saudi Arabia** questioned the process led by **Australia** ⚠️ according to Climate Home [3]. **The African Group (54 countries)** asked whether process reform is linked to the final decision 🟡 [11].
3. **Parties working on Article 6.2** (bilateral trading of carbon credits between countries) discussed who pays for the technical expert review and the international registry 🟡 [11]. **The European Union** wants usage-based fees; **the LMDCs** want waivers for all developing countries 🟡 [11]. The text goes to the CMA (the meeting of the Parties to the Paris Agreement) 🟡 [11].
4. **Philanthropic donors** pledged more than USD 480 million for methane and other potent greenhouse gases at the summit co-hosted by **China, the United Kingdom and Brazil** ✅ [8][12]. **Monique Barbut, French Minister for Ecological Transition**, wants methane in the final decision ⚠️ according to Climate Home and Health Policy Watch [3][8].
5. **69 mayors**, representing more than 250 million people, committed to further climate action ✅ [12]. **Anacláudia Rossbach, UN-Habitat Executive Director**, said "housing is climate action" ✅ [12].
6. **Matthew Wale, Prime Minister of Solomon Islands**, announced USD 400,000 for the next replenishment of the GCF (the UN Green Climate Fund) ✅ [5][12]. **Türkiye** signed a USD 1.6 billion financing agreement with the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank (AIIB) ✅ [12].
7. **Rifat Hisarcıklıoğlu, President of TOBB (Union of Chambers and Commodity Exchanges of Türkiye) and COP31 Private Sector Envoy**, called for common carbon measurement rules and fewer repeated data requests ⚠️ single source (Anadolu) [7].

## Negotiations: where things stand
| Topic | What moved | What is stuck | Texts released |
|---|---|---|---|
| Final decision ("cover decision") and process reform | **Presidency** consults heads of delegation on UNFCCC reform; **Environmental Integrity Group (EIG)** proposes COPs every 2-3 years and caps on delegation size 🟡 [11]. **Australia** takes written inputs until Saturday ✅ [12] | **LMDCs** against "unnecessary" decisions 🟡 [11]; **Russia and Saudi Arabia** question the process ⚠️ [3] | None; first draft expected over the weekend ⚠️ [3] |
| Finance | **Climate finance work programme**: broad support for tripling adaptation finance as a topic 🟡 [11] | Disagreement on scope: only Art. 9.1 (developed countries' obligations) or all of Art. 9 including private sources 🟡 [11]. **Art. 2.1(c)** (aligning financial flows): the **Arab Group** rejects the Veredas Dialogue report 🟡 [11] | First draft in preparation; compilation text for Art. 2.1(c) 🟡 [11] |
| Article 6 (carbon markets) | Draft CMA text on Art. 6.2 fees forwarded to the CMA 🟡 [11] | Waivers for all developing countries (LMDCs) vs usage-based fees (EU) 🟡 [11] | Draft CMA decision 🟡 [11] |
| Mitigation | **AOSIS (small island states)** accepts the informal note as a basis 🟡 [11] | **LMDCs**: no link to the Global Stocktake and no mitigation committee 🟡 [11] | Co-facilitators' informal note 🟡 [11] |
| Adaptation / GGA (Global Goal on Adaptation) | Adaptation communications: agreement "close", submissions by September 2027 🟡 [11] | GGA: **G77/China** wants finance in the text; **EU, UK, Canada** refer it to the finance work programme 🟡 [11] | Revised drafts with options 🟡 [11] |
| Just transition | Broad consensus on extending the work programme 🟡 [11] | Duration, scope, food systems 🟡 [11] | — |
| Trade / unilateral measures | **Arab Group and African Group** oppose unilateral trade measures (such as CBAM, the EU carbon border levy) in the just transition debate 🟡 [11] | No trade item on the agenda 🟡 [11] | — |
| Methane | **France**, **Barbados** and **Germany** want methane in the text ⚠️ [3][8] | No agreed wording ⚠️ [3] | — |
