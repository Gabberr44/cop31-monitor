# High-level dialogue on urban resilience, adaptation and climate action
**When:** 12/11, 13:30–17:00 Antalya (11:30–15:00 Italian time) · **Where:** Leaders' Summit venue, Antalya EXPO Center · **Organiser:** COP31 Presidency and UN-Habitat ✅ [1][3]<br>
**Speakers:** **Anacláudia Rossbach** (Executive Director, UN-Habitat; she also delivered António Guterres's message); **Sagar Dhakal** (Member of Parliament, Nepal); mayors of Addis Ababa, London and Melbourne [2]<br>
🎥 Video not yet available

## In brief
It is the first high-level dialogue on urban resilience at a UN climate conference ✅ [2]. Cities want more finance: today they receive about one fifth of what they need ✅ [2].

## Key points
- **Anacláudia Rossbach, UN-Habitat Executive Director**, called for "systemic change" in urban planning ✅ [2].
- **UN-Habitat** reports that 80% of national climate plans now have well-developed urban measures, up from 50% in the previous round ✅ [2].
- **69 mayors**, representing more than 250 million people, committed to further action: +60% on last year ✅ [2]. Examples: flood warnings in Addis Ababa, London's first heat plan, incentives for 400 commercial buildings in Melbourne ✅ [2].
- **Seychelles** warned that recovery from storms and floods can trap small countries in debt ✅ [2].

## Announcements and figures
- **UN-Habitat**: cities need about USD 4.5 trillion a year in climate investment through 2030 ✅ [2].
- **UN**: a new platform aims to unlock more than USD 3 billion to protect 75 million people from climate shocks by 2030 ✅ [2].

## Quotes
- "Housing is climate action. Urban planning is climate action." — **Anacláudia Rossbach, UN-Habitat Executive Director** [2]

## Relevance for the VSME
**Low** — No obligation for companies. Municipal heat and flood plans are, however, a useful source for an SME assessing physical risks at its sites.
