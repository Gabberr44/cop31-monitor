# Methane and non-CO₂ greenhouse gases
**When:** 12/11, 09:00–10:30 Antalya (07:00–08:30 Italian time) for the leaders-level non-CO₂ summit · **Where:** Meeting Room-3 (Topkapı) · **Organiser:** United Kingdom ✅ [1]<br>
**Speakers:** **Monique Barbut** (Environment Minister, France); **Mia Mottley** (Prime Minister, Barbados); **Chris Bowen** (Climate Minister, Australia, and COP31 President of Negotiations) — remarks reported by Climate Home, venue not specified [2]<br>
🎥 Video not yet available

## In brief
Several governments called for stronger rules on methane, a gas that warms far more than CO₂ in the short term. The topic matters because it could enter the COP31 final decision.

## Key points
- **Monique Barbut, French Environment Minister**, called for a strong mention of methane in the final decision ("cover decision") ⚠️ according to Climate Home [2].
- **Monique Barbut** asked oil and gas companies to bring methane intensity close to zero by 2030 and to end flaring and venting ⚠️ [2].
- **Mia Mottley, Prime Minister of Barbados**, proposed a legally binding global methane treaty ⚠️ according to Climate Home [2]. As a first step she suggested a coalition-of-the-willing summit ⚠️ [2].
- **Chris Bowen, Australian Climate Minister**, said Australia will sign a voluntary "near-zero" methane pledge for fossil fuels, despite pressure from farmers ⚠️ according to Climate Home [2].
- **CCAC (Climate and Clean Air Coalition), UNEP and UNICEF** estimated that wastewater and sanitation produce 7–10% of global methane ⚠️ according to Commonspace [3].

## Relevance for the VSME
**Medium** — In the VSME (module B3), methane is part of Scope 1 emissions in tCO₂e: SMEs with gas boilers, network leaks or wastewater treatment may get more precise questions.
