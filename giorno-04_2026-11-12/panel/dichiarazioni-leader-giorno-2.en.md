# Leaders' Summit, day 2: national statements
**When:** 12/11, 09:00–17:00 Antalya (07:00–15:00 Italian time); closing 17:00–17:30 Antalya (15:00–15:30 Italian time) · **Where:** Plenary Hall-1 (Anadolu) · **Organiser:** COP31 Presidency and UNFCCC ✅ [1]<br>
**Speakers (selection):** **Andy Burnham** (Prime Minister, United Kingdom); **Matthew Wale** (Prime Minister, Solomon Islands); **Greta Thunberg** (activist)<br>
🎥 Video not yet available

## In brief
On the second day of the summit, leaders delivered their national statements. The main announcements concern climate security, private finance for clean energy and the GCF (the UN Green Climate Fund).

## Key points
- **Andy Burnham, UK Prime Minister**, appointed **Dame Madeline Alessandri** as the UK's first special representative for climate security ✅ [2].
- **Andy Burnham** said the shift to clean energy is the only route to energy security ✅ [2].
- **Andy Burnham** announced backing for **Amplify**, which aims at £5 billion of new private finance for clean energy projects ✅ [2].
- **Andy Burnham** pledged to put climate resilience at the centre of next year's UK G20 presidency, with debt, development and insurance ✅ [2].
- **Matthew Wale, Prime Minister of Solomon Islands**, announced USD 400,000 for the next GCF replenishment ✅ [3]. It is the first pledge from a small island state that is also a least developed country ✅ [3].
- **Greta Thunberg, Swedish activist**, said leaders are "completely detached from reality" ⚠️ according to Climate Home [4].

## Announcements and numbers
- **Green Climate Fund**: USD 22.3 billion committed in more than 130 developing countries, of which USD 1.3 billion in Pacific island states ✅ [3].
- **United Kingdom, Norway and Canada**: partnership to mobilise USD 10 billion in climate finance by 2030 ⚠️ according to Climate Home [4].

## Quotes
- "Climate is a national security issue." — **Andy Burnham, UK Prime Minister** [2]
- "Solomon Islands has set the bar; now it is time for others to follow the lead." — **Mafalda Duarte, GCF Executive Director** [3]

## Relevance for the VSME
**Low** — Policy and public-finance announcements with no new obligations for companies; the push for private finance confirms that banks and investors will ask for more climate data.
