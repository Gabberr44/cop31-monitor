# COP31 Business Forum: opening plenary
**When:** 12/11, 10:00–12:30 Antalya (08:00–10:30 Italian time) · **Where:** Plenary Hall-2 (Akdeniz) · **Organiser:** TOBB (Union of Chambers and Commodity Exchanges of Türkiye), COP31 Private Sector Envoy, COP31 Presidency ✅ [1]<br>
**Speakers:** **Rifat Hisarcıklıoğlu** (President of TOBB and COP31 Private Sector Envoy) [2]; other speakers to be confirmed<br>
🎥 Video not yet available

## In brief
The plenary opens the COP31 Business and Investment Summit, which continues on 13 November. It matters for SMEs because business asked for common carbon measurement rules and fewer repeated data requests.

## Key points
- **Rifat Hisarcıklıoğlu, President of TOBB**, said climate goals are met in factories and construction sites, not in negotiating rooms ⚠️ [2].
- **Rifat Hisarcıklıoğlu** called for cheaper finance and local-currency loans, recalling that 9 in 10 companies worldwide are SMEs ⚠️ [2].
- **Rifat Hisarcıklıoğlu** asked that climate rules not become new trade barriers ⚠️ [2]. He proposed common rules to measure carbon and recognition of carbon costs already paid in another country ⚠️ [2].
- **Rifat Hisarcıklıoğlu** asked that the same product be measured the same way wherever it is made ⚠️ [2]. He also asked that companies not have to report the same information repeatedly ⚠️ [2].
- **Rifat Hisarcıklıoğlu** called for markets for clean products, for example through public procurement and long-term purchase contracts ⚠️ [2].
- **TOBB** coordinated seven working groups, including one on SMEs, which produced a joint declaration on barriers to climate investment ⚠️ [2].

## Announcements and numbers
- **Rifat Hisarcıklıoğlu** backed the goal of raising electricity to 35% of final energy consumption by 2035 ("35-by-35") ⚠️ [2].

## Relevance for the VSME
**Medium** — Not a negotiated decision, but the call for common metrics and "report once" is exactly the purpose of the VSME (EFRAG's voluntary standard for SMEs).
