# Pacific Resilience Facility: pledging event
**When:** 12/11, 15:30–17:00 Antalya (13:30–15:00 Italian time) · **Where:** Leaders' Summit venue · **Organiser:** Australia, Pacific islands, Türkiye ✅ [1]<br>
**Speakers:** **Surangel Whipps Jr** (President, Palau); **Anthony Albanese** (Prime Minister, Australia); **Sakiasi Ditoka** (Foreign Minister, Fiji) [2]<br>
🎥 Video not yet available

## In brief
The event aimed to raise money for the Pacific Resilience Facility, the Pacific-led regional fund that finances small community resilience projects. No donor country brought new money.

## Key points
- **The Netherlands, the United Kingdom, Canada and Ireland** re-announced pledges they had already made public ⚠️ according to Climate Home [2].
- **Minderoo Foundation (Australian foundation)** announced USD 5 million ⚠️ [2].
- **Climate Home** puts the fund at over USD 200 million, about USD 300 million short of its 2026 target of USD 500 million ⚠️ [2]. **Yahoo News Australia** presents the same figure as a "USD 200 million milestone" ⚠️ [3].
- **Australia (USD 67 million)** and **Saudi Arabia (USD 50 million)** are the largest donors ⚠️ [2].
- **Sakiasi Ditoka, Fiji's Foreign Minister**, hopes for new money by the end of COP31 ⚠️ [2].

## Quotes
- "We are putting our own skin in the game." — **Surangel Whipps Jr, President of Palau** [2]

## Relevance for the VSME
**Low** — It concerns public adaptation finance in the Pacific; no direct effect on European SME reporting.
