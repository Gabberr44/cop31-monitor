# Closing of the subsidiary bodies and midway stocktake
**When:** 14/11, afternoon and evening Antalya (exact time n/a) · **Where:** Antalya EXPO Center, plenaries · **Organiser:** UNFCCC, COP31 Presidencies (Türkiye and Australia)<br>
**Speakers:** **Adonia Ayebare** (SBSTA Chair); **Julia Gardiner** (SBI Chair); **Murat Kurum** (COP31 President, Türkiye); **Chris Bowen** (President of Negotiations, Australia); **Simon Stiell** (UNFCCC Executive Secretary)<br>
🎥 Video not yet available

## In brief
The Convention's technical bodies closed their one-week session; in the evening the Presidencies took stock before ministers arrive. It matters because from Monday open issues move from the technical to the political level.

## Key points
- **SBSTA (the body for scientific and technological advice) and SBI (the body for implementation)** close their 65th session (SB65), opened on 9/11, with 46 agenda items ✅ [2].
- **UNFCCC** scheduled the SB closing plenaries and the COP/CMP/CMA plenaries (the assemblies of the Convention, the Kyoto Protocol and the Paris Agreement) for the afternoon ✅ [3].
- **The COP31 Presidencies (Türkiye and Australia)** called an evening stocktaking plenary, where countries' first "red lines" are expected to emerge ⚠️ according to Climate Home [1].
- **Simon Stiell, UNFCCC Executive Secretary**, called progress "constructive" and urged tackling the hardest issues swiftly ⚠️ according to Climate Home [1].
- **Chris Bowen, President of Negotiations**, spoke of "really good and positive conversations", but some issues need more time ⚠️ according to AFP [4].
- **The plenary outcomes** have no written account yet at 22:45 (Italian time): to be checked tomorrow morning.

## Quotes
- "No single COP can magic this crisis away, but each must drive progress" — **Simon Stiell, UNFCCC Executive Secretary** [4]

## Relevance for the VSME
**Low** — a procedural step; effects for companies depend on the texts released next week.
