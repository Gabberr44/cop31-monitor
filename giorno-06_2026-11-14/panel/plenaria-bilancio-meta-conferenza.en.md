# Closing of the subsidiary bodies and the second-week plan
**When:** 14/11, from the afternoon until 00:48 on 15/11 Antalya (22:48 Italian time on 14/11) · **Where:** Antalya EXPO Center, plenaries · **Organiser:** UNFCCC, COP31 Presidencies (Türkiye and Australia)<br>
**Speakers:** **Murat Kurum** (COP31 President, Türkiye); **Adonia Ayebare** (SBSTA Chair); **Julia Gardiner** (SBI Chair); **Simon Stiell** (UNFCCC Executive Secretary)<br>
🎥 Video not yet available

## In brief
The Convention's technical bodies closed their session after midnight and forwarded almost all hard files to ministers. The Presidency explained how the second week will work: it matters because from Monday the choices become political.

## Key points
- **Murat Kurum, COP31 President**, announced four work streams: ministerial engagement, Presidency consultations, ministerial consultations on outstanding political issues, technical negotiations 🟡 [1].
- **Murat Kurum** confirmed five pairs of facilitating ministers: mitigation (Germany-India), finance (France-Philippines), adaptation (Sierra Leone-UK), just transition (Mexico-Poland), Ocean (Canada-Palau) 🟡 [1].
- **Russia, the Arab Group, the African Group, the LMDCs (Like-Minded Developing Countries, including China and India) and Argentina** complain of unclear mandates for the Presidency consultations and oppose a "cover decision" (the final political text) on reforming the UN process 🟡 [1].
- **The Arab Group** said it will not accept top-down global targets or targets for specific sectors or energy sources; **Switzerland** stressed limiting 1.5 °C overshoot 🟡 [1].
- **SBSTA (the body for scientific and technological advice) and SBI (the body for implementation)** adopted their reports and closed at 00:48 on 15/11 (Antalya time) 🟡 [1].
- **Simon Stiell, UNFCCC Executive Secretary**, had called progress "constructive" and urged moving swiftly to the hardest issues ⚠️ according to Climate Home [2].

## Announcements and numbers
- **5** pairs of facilitating ministers and **4** work streams for the second week 🟡 [1].
- **Agriculture**: the decision slips to 2027 (SBI 66) 🟡 [1].

## Quotes
- "No single COP can magic this crisis away, but each must drive progress" — **Simon Stiell, UNFCCC Executive Secretary** [3]

## Relevance for the VSME
**Low** — a procedural step; effects on companies depend on the texts ministers close next week.
