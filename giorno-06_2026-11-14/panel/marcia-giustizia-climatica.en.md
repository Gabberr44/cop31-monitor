# The climate justice march
**When:** 14/11, daytime (time n/a) · **Where:** Antalya, from Aydın Kanza Park to Republic Square (about 1 km) · **Organiser:** more than 100 environmental groups, trade unions, rights associations and opposition parties<br>
**Speakers:** **Halime Saman** (People's Climate Summit platform); **Nil Ormanlı** (organising committee); **Luisa Neubauer** (Fridays for Future); **Claudia Roth** (former German Minister of State for Culture)<br>
🎥 Video not available (unofficial event)

## In brief
Thousands marched in Antalya at the conference midpoint, demanding climate finance, a fossil fuel phase-out and respect for rights. It matters as a political signal, in a country that often bans large demonstrations.

## Key points
- **Turkish police** estimate more than 7,000 participants ⚠️ according to AFP [1]; **organisers** more than 10,000 ⚠️ according to dpa [2]. It is the largest demonstration since the COP began 🟡 [1][2].
- **Turkish authorities** authorised the march; participants were checked by police at the entrance 🟡 [1][2].
- **Halime Saman, People's Climate Summit**, criticised Türkiye for having no roadmap to phase out fossil fuels ⚠️ according to AFP [1].
- **Luisa Neubauer, Fridays for Future**, warned that local participants risk retaliation once foreign activists and journalists leave ⚠️ according to dpa [2].
- **The People's Climate Summit**, with hundreds of Turkish and international groups, opened in Antalya the same weekend ⚠️ according to dpa [2].

## Quotes
- "We are suffering the consequences of climate injustice, yet our voices are never heard at the COP" — **Halime Saman, People's Climate Summit** [1]
