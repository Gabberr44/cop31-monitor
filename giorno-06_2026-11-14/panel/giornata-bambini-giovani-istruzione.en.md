# Children, Youth, Education and Skills Day
**When:** 14/11, all day (times of individual events n/a) · **Where:** Antalya EXPO Center, Blue Zone and Green Zone · **Organiser:** COP31 Presidency, with UNICEF and Turkish ministries<br>
**Speakers:** **Paolo Marchi** (UNICEF Representative in Türkiye); **Tûba Büyüküstün** (UNICEF Türkiye Goodwill Ambassador); **Zehra Güneş** (UNICEF Türkiye Child Rights Advocate)<br>
🎥 Video not yet available

## In brief
The Presidency devoted the day to children, youth, education and green skills. For companies it matters mainly on skills: one session discussed finance, industrial decarbonisation and green jobs for young people.

## Key points
- **UNICEF and Türkiye's Ministry of Family and Social Services** organised "Rights of the Next Generation": young people presented the "Children's Call to Action on Climate" ✅ [1].
- **The session "Green Jobs for a Green Economy"** brought together finance, industry and international organisations on climate finance, green skills and jobs for young people ✅ [1].
- **The COP31 Presidency** held the "Antalya Dialogue on Children, Youth and Climate Resilience", with UNICEF taking part ✅ [1].
- **The Youth-led Climate Forum** closed in the morning ✅ [2].
- **Türkiye** is the 100th member country of UNESCO's Greening Education Partnership, as announced in October by **Education Minister Yusuf Tekin** ✅ [3].

## Quotes
- "Climate change is one of the greatest threats to children's rights, wellbeing and futures" — **Paolo Marchi, UNICEF Representative in Türkiye** [1]
