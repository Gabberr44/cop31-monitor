# Just transition mechanism: the informal note
**When:** note circulated late on Friday 13/11; reactions on 14/11 · **Where:** informal consultations, Antalya EXPO Center · **Organiser:** Australian Presidency of Negotiations<br>
**Speakers:** **Chris Bowen** (President of Negotiations, Australia); **Anabella Rosemberg** (Climate Action Network International); **Romain Ioualalen** (Oil Change International); **Amiera Sawas** (Fossil Fuel Treaty Initiative)<br>
🎥 Video not planned (closed consultations)

## In brief
An informal note proposes how to make the just transition mechanism work (the "Belém-Antalya mechanism" agreed at COP30). It matters because it is one of COP31's expected outcomes, but for now it brings no new finance.

## Key points
- **The informal note** proposes a two-tier structure: one government-led and one implementation arm, with civil society, hosted or supported by the UN climate secretariat ⚠️ according to Climate Home [1].
- **The note** mainly foresees a knowledge hub, reports, information-gathering and an annual meeting ⚠️ [1].
- **On finance** it only foresees "matchmaking of requests with existing support" ⚠️ [1].
- **Anabella Rosemberg, Climate Action Network International**, notes the absence of the formal body with a finance taskforce that developing countries demanded ⚠️ [1].
- **Romain Ioualalen, Oil Change International**, accuses the Australian Presidency of watering down the text ⚠️ [1].
- **Chris Bowen, President of Negotiations**, sees "strong interest" in an ambitious outcome and calls the draft "comprehensive" ⚠️ [1].
- **The subsidiary bodies (SBSTA and SBI)** forwarded the just transition work programme to CMA 8 (the Paris Agreement assembly), taking note of the co-chairs' informal note 🟡 [2].
- **Murat Kurum, COP31 President**, entrusted ministerial consultations on just transition to **Alicia Bárcena (Mexico)** and **Krzysztof Bolesta (Poland)** 🟡 [2].

## Quotes
- "Justice needs structures, not endless hollow words" — **Amiera Sawas, Fossil Fuel Treaty Initiative** [1]

## Relevance for the VSME
**Low** — it concerns cooperation between states; no new obligation for companies.
