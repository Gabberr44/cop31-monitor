# Carbon markets (Article 6) and greenhouse gas data
**When:** 14/11, informal consultations and SB closing plenary (until 00:48 on 15/11 Antalya, 22:48 Italian time on 14/11) · **Where:** Antalya EXPO Center · **Organiser:** CMA (the Conference of the Parties to the Paris Agreement), SBSTA, SBI<br>
**Speakers:** **Malin Ahlberg** (Germany) and **Sonam Tashi** (Bhutan), Art. 6.4 co-facilitators; **Adonia Ayebare** (SBSTA Chair)<br>
🎥 Video not planned (informal consultations)

## In brief
Parties discussed how the UN carbon crediting mechanism (Art. 6.4) works in practice and failed to agree on the UN emissions data interface. It matters because this shapes the quality of credits companies can buy and the availability of national data used as reference.

## Key points
- **The LMDCs (Like-Minded Developing Countries, including China and India)** fear financial difficulties put the Art. 6.4 mechanism at risk and call for urgent resources 🟡 [1].
- **The Least Developed Countries (LDCs)** report that some approved methodologies, for example on clean cooking, are hard for project developers to apply 🟡 [1]. They propose that the Supervisory Body assess how they work and report to CMA 9 🟡 [1].
- **Ukraine** warns that the current approach favours large projects and makes small ones financially unviable 🟡 [1].
- **Co-facilitators Ahlberg and Tashi** will prepare a draft CMA decision on Art. 6.4 🟡 [1].
- **The SBI (the body for implementation)** forwarded Art. 6.2 funding arrangements (bilateral trading of credits between countries) to CMA 8; **the SBSTA** forwarded Art. 6.8 (non-market approaches) with a heavily bracketed text 🟡 [1].
- **Adonia Ayebare, SBSTA Chair**, reported no agreement on the greenhouse gas data interface: the item returns to SBSTA 66 (June 2027) 🟡 [1]. **The Arab Group and Russia** object to new functions; **the EU, the Umbrella Group and the Pacific islands** want the agreed modules published 🟡 [1].

## Announcements and numbers
- **Art. 6.4**: draft CMA decision expected in the second week 🟡 [1].
- **GHG data interface**: postponed to June 2027 🟡 [1].

## Relevance for the VSME
**Medium** — an SME that buys carbon credits must report them separately from B3 (energy and greenhouse gas) emissions: clearer Art. 6.4 rules help it choose reliable credits.
