# Italy launches the CLEAR Fund
**When:** 17/11, time n/a · **Where:** MASE–SEforALL event, Antalya · **Organiser:** MASE (Italian Ministry of Environment and Energy Security) and SEforALL (Sustainable Energy for All)<br>
**Speakers:** **Gilberto Pichetto Fratin** (Minister of Environment and Energy Security)<br>
🎥 Video not available (no official recording found as of 18/11/2026)

## At a glance
Italy presented the CLEAR Fund, a climate finance fund based on Article 6.2 of the Paris Agreement. Article 6.2 lets countries trade emission reductions achieved through projects abroad.

## Key points
- **MASE and SEforALL** lead the fund, with technical support from **ISPRA (Italy's Institute for Environmental Protection and Research)** ⚠️ according to ADI News [1].
- **The fund** will help partner countries strengthen rules, registries and measurement, reporting and verification systems before any trading ⚠️ [1].
- **The pilot projects** cover Cuba, Ethiopia and Tanzania ⚠️ [1].
- **Gilberto Pichetto Fratin** said the fund aims to act as a "catalyst to attract new capital" ⚠️ [1].
- **The ADI News article** does not report the fund's size ⚠️ [1].

## Relevance for the VSME
**Medium** — It creates no obligations for SMEs. But it shows Italy will use Art. 6.2 markets: companies considering carbon credits must keep them separate from their own emissions in the VSME.
