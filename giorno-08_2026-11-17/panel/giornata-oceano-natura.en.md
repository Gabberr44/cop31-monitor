# Ocean and nature day
**When:** 17/11, all day · **Where:** blue zone and pavilions, Antalya EXPO Center · **Organiser:** COP31 Presidency and various organisers<br>
**Speakers:** **Marine Lecerf** (Ocean and Climate Platform); **Joanna Post** (UNESCO Intergovernmental Oceanographic Commission); **Brianna Fruean** (Pacific youth climate envoy); **Hindou Oumarou Ibrahim** (UN Sustainable Development Goals Advocate); **Amy Duchelle** (FAO); **Roberto Chang López** (Vice Minister of Finance, Honduras)<br>
🎥 Video not yet available

## At a glance
The Turkish Presidency's thematic day was devoted to ocean, seas, nature and land use. The ocean features prominently in the first draft of the final decision, but it may not stay there ✅ [1].

## Key points
- **An analysis cited by UN News** finds that more than 95% of coastal and island countries have ocean measures in their NDC (national climate plan); about 60% depend on external finance ✅ [1].
- **Marine Lecerf, Head of International Policy at the Ocean and Climate Platform**, said what is needed is not a new mandate but acceleration ✅ [1]. She opposes linking carbon dioxide removal techniques to the ocean ⚠️ according to Climate Home [2].
- **Joanna Post (UNESCO)** recalled that only about 28% of the seafloor has been mapped ✅ [1].
- **Türkiye** presented the Marine Litter Action Platform; **the European Union** joined France and Brazil's Blue NDC Challenge ✅ [1].
- **23 countries** endorsed the Antalya Action Plan on Integrated Fire Management, which focuses on prevention ✅ [1].
- **Germanwatch** published the Climate Risk Index 2027: Dominica, Myanmar and Honduras hardest hit over 30 years; for 2025 alone Jamaica (Hurricane Melissa) ranks first, followed by Sri Lanka and Spain ✅ according to UN News [1].

## Announcements and numbers
- Extreme events 1996–2025: more than 916,000 deaths and over 5.5 billion people affected; heatwaves cause 40% of deaths ✅ according to Germanwatch, reported by UN News [1].
- A new initiative to restore 30 million hectares of peatlands and mobilise USD 100 billion ✅ [1].
- More than USD 30 million announced for coral reef projects in 22 developing countries ✅ [1].
- The Blue NDC Challenge has 27 members and a new taskforce backed by more than 20 organisations ⚠️ according to Climate Home [2].

## Quotes
- "We're not asking for a new mandate, we're asking for acceleration." — **Marine Lecerf, Ocean and Climate Platform** [1]
