# Technical negotiations: Article 6, finance, just transition
**When:** 17/11, all day · **Where:** informal consultations and contact groups, Antalya EXPO Center · **Organiser:** UNFCCC, COP31 Presidency of Negotiations<br>
**Speakers:** co-facilitators **Cristina Andrea Figueroa Vargas** (Chile) and **Peer Stiansen** (Norway) on Art. 6.2; **Malin Ahlberg** (Germany) and **Sonam Tashi** (Bhutan) on Art. 6.4; **Amena Yauvoli** on the Standing Committee on Finance; **Federica Fricano** (Italy) and **Joseph Teo** (Singapore) on just transition<br>
🎥 No video expected (informal consultations)

## At a glance
Technical negotiations were meant to wrap up, but according to ENB "agreement was nowhere to be found" ⚠️ [1]. Many texts were revised; political choices move to ministers and the Presidencies.

## Key points
- **Art. 6.2 (trading of reductions between countries):** countries are discussing how to fund registries and technical reviews. **The EU** wants fee waivers only for least developed countries and small islands; **AILAC (Latin American countries) and the Arab Group** want waivers for all developing countries ⚠️ according to ENB [1].
- **Art. 6.4 (UN crediting mechanism):** **the EU**, with **Ukraine**, wants faster work on conflict-of-interest rules for Supervisory Body members and consultants ⚠️ [1].
- **Finance:** **the EU** proposes a biennial report on Art. 2.1(c), i.e. aligning finance flows with the Paris goals; **LDCs (Least Developed Countries), the Arab Group and AILAC** oppose ⚠️ [1].
- **Just transition:** **China** proposes a dedicated body, which **the EU** calls a "red line"; the EU also opposes new finance obligations ⚠️ [1].
- **Climate finance work programme:** **the poorest countries** want it extended by two years with a goal of tripling adaptation finance; **the EU and Switzerland** want no text ⚠️ according to Climate Home [2].
- **Technology:** **the G77/China (the developing-country bloc)** wants "at least" USD 106.2 million budget for the UN Climate Technology Centre; **the EU** opposes ⚠️ [1].

## Quotes
- "A weak or procedural result only, or worse, no text at all, would represent a failure." — **Climate Action Network International** [2]

## Relevance for the VSME
**Low** — These are rules between states. The Art. 2.1(c) report, if approved, would over time strengthen banks' attention to their clients' climate data.
