# Final decision: the draft under fire
**When:** 17/11, time n/a (consultations lasted over four hours) · **Where:** Presidency consultations, Antalya EXPO Center · **Organiser:** COP31 Presidency of Negotiations (Australia)<br>
**Speakers:** **Sally Box** (co-facilitator, COP31 Presidency of Negotiations); interventions by **Wopke Hoekstra** (EU Climate Commissioner), **Ilana Seid** (AOSIS chair), negotiating groups<br>
🎥 No video expected (closed consultations)

## At a glance
Countries kept commenting on the 18-page draft "cover decision", COP31's final political text. The European Union finds it too broad, oil producers reject roadmaps and sectoral targets: a compromise is still far off.

## Key points
- **Wopke Hoekstra, EU Climate Commissioner**, said the draft has "many of the characteristics of a shopping list" and "needs significant change" 🟡 [2][3]. He wants a text focused on the implementation gap ⚠️ according to Climate Home [2].
- **Chris Bowen, COP31 President of Negotiations**, announced "at least one more draft" before the end of the week 🟡 [3][4].
- **The Environmental Integrity Group (EIG, including Switzerland, Mexico and South Korea)** wants the text to address the 1.5°C overshoot and accelerate the transition away from fossil fuels ⚠️ according to ENB [1].
- **The Arab Group** questioned the legitimacy of the process and rejected roadmaps, sectoral targets and the International Court of Justice advisory opinion ⚠️ [1].
- **The African Group** wants finance that does not add to debt; **the Least Developed Countries (LDCs) and Malaysia** stressed the adaptation finance gap ⚠️ [1].
- **Ilana Seid, chair of AOSIS (small island states)**, called any delay to IPCC (the UN climate science panel) reports "unjustifiable" 🟡 [2][3]. **Saudi Arabia** said it is not seeking to delay science 🟡 [2][3].

## Announcements and numbers
- **The Presidency's official draft** (16/11, 144 paragraphs) calls on developed countries to phase out unilateral climate-related trade measures by the end of 2027 (paragraph 119) ✅ [5]. CBAM (the EU mechanism that puts a carbon price on certain imported goods) is not named ✅ [5].
- **The same draft** calls for raising electricity to 35% of final energy consumption by 2035 and adaptation finance to at least USD 120 billion ✅ [5].
- According to ENB, cover-decision consultations lasted more than four hours "with little progress to show for it" ⚠️ [1].
- **Spain and Vanuatu** want the decision to refer to the Brazilian COP30 Presidency's global fossil fuel roadmap; the draft only mentions it in paragraph 140 ⚠️ according to Climate Home [2].

## Quotes
- "Many of the characteristics of a shopping list." — **Wopke Hoekstra, EU Climate Commissioner** [2][3]

## Relevance for the VSME
**High** — 35% electrification and trade measures such as CBAM touch the energy and emissions data (VSME B3) that customers, banks and importers ask SMEs for.
