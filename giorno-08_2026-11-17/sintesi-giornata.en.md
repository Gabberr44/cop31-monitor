# COP31 — Day 8 · Tuesday 17 November 2026
✅ Final version — consolidated on 18/11/2026 at 09:55 (Italian time)<br>
First evening draft: 17/11/2026 at 22:45 (Italian time).

Theme of the day chosen by the **COP31 Presidency (Türkiye)**: "Ocean, Seas, Nature and Land Use" ✅ [1]. The resumed high-level segment began, with ministers' statements (17–18 November) ✅ [11].

## At a glance
- **What happened:** **Wopke Hoekstra, EU Climate Commissioner**, called the draft final decision a "shopping list" that needs significant change 🟡 [3][4]. Ministers started working on the text; a new draft is expected this week 🟡 [4][13].
- **Why it matters:** the official draft contains points with direct effects on companies, such as 35% electrification and a call to remove "unilateral" climate-related trade measures by 2027 ✅ [14].
- **Tomorrow:** on Wednesday 18 the ministers' statements end ✅ [11]; the Australian Presidency of Negotiations hears countries' reactions to the draft 🟡 according to Reuters [13].

## Key facts
1. **Wopke Hoekstra, European Climate Commissioner**, told ministers the draft has "many of the characteristics of a shopping list" and "needs significant change" 🟡 [3][4]. He asked COP31 to send "the strongest possible signal" on the shift to clean energy 🟡 according to Reuters [13].
2. **Chris Bowen, COP31 President of Negotiations (Australia)**, acknowledged the text contains elements "that not all parties agree on" 🟡 [4][13]. He announced "at least one more draft" before the end of the week 🟡 [4][13]. **João Paulo Capobianco, Brazil's Environment Minister**, said deadlocks can be overcome through "an adjustment in the wording" ⚠️ according to AFP [5].
3. **The Presidency's official draft (16/11, 144 paragraphs)** calls on developed countries to phase out unilateral climate-related trade measures by the end of 2027 (paragraph 119) ✅ [14]. The text does not name CBAM (the EU mechanism that puts a carbon price on certain imported goods) ✅ [14]. If it stays in the final text, the paragraph would however increase political pressure on CBAM too. The same draft calls for raising electricity to 35% of final energy consumption by 2035 ✅ [14].
4. **The European Union** backs the voluntary "35-by-35" electrification goal 🟡 [2][3]. **The Arab Group** instead rejected references to roadmaps, "top-down" sectoral targets and the International Court of Justice opinion ⚠️ according to ENB [2].
5. **Ilana Seid, Chair of AOSIS (the Alliance of Small Island States)**, called any delay to the next IPCC (the UN climate science panel) reports "unjustifiable" 🟡 [3][4]. **Saudi Arabia's delegation** replied that it does not seek to delay science but wants adequate review time 🟡 [3][4].
6. **Cardinal Pietro Parolin, Vatican Secretary of State**, opened the high-level segment ✅ [11]. In **Pope Leo's** message he said the climate crisis is "being overshadowed by geopolitical tensions" ⚠️ according to Reuters [13].
7. **Gilberto Pichetto Fratin, Italy's Minister of Environment and Energy Security**, told the plenary that "climate action also requires adequate resources" ⚠️ according to Radiocor [8]. He cited €5.8 billion of Italian public climate finance in 2022–2025 ⚠️ [8]. With **SEforALL (Sustainable Energy for All)** he presented the CLEAR Fund, based on Article 6.2 (trading of reductions between countries) ⚠️ [9].
8. **UN News (UN)** reports that more than 95% of coastal and island countries include ocean measures in their NDCs (national emission-reduction plans) ✅ [6]. **Germanwatch (German NGO)** presented the Climate Risk Index 2027: more than 916,000 deaths from extreme events in 1996–2025 ✅ according to UN News [6].
9. **Denmark** is the fifth country to present a national fossil-fuel exit roadmap ⚠️ according to Climate Home [3]. **Mafalda Duarte, Executive Director of the Green Climate Fund (GCF)**, asked rich countries to lead the fund's replenishment ⚠️ according to Climate Home [7].

## Negotiations: where things stand
| Topic | What moved | What is stuck | Texts released |
|---|---|---|---|
| Final decision ("cover decision") | Consultations led by **Sally Box (Presidency of Negotiations)**; **AOSIS** and the **Environmental Integrity Group (EIG)** want a fossil-fuel exit ⚠️ [2] | **Arab Group**: no to roadmaps and sectoral targets ⚠️ [2]; **EU**: text too broad 🟡 [3][4] | Draft of 16/11 (18 pages) ✅ [14]; new draft expected this week 🟡 [4][13] |
| Mitigation / NDCs | Draft: 35% electricity by 2035; net zero by 2030 for developed countries ✅ [14] | Two fossil-fuel formulas ("transition" or "phase out"), both at risk 🟡 [13][14] | New version of the periodic review text ⚠️ [2] |
| Trade / unilateral measures | Draft: phase out unilateral measures by end-2027 (para. 119) ✅ [14] | No official EU reaction to the paragraph found in direct sources | Presidency draft ✅ [14] |
| Article 6 (carbon markets) | Revised 6.2 text (trading between countries); **EU**: stricter conflict-of-interest rules in 6.4 ⚠️ [2] | Fees to fund registries and reviews ⚠️ [2] | Revised drafts 6.2, 6.4, 6.8 ⚠️ [2] |
| Finance | **EU**: biennial report on Art. 2.1(c) (aligning finance flows) ⚠️ [2] | **LDCs (Least Developed Countries), Arab Group, AILAC (Latin American alliance)** opposed ⚠️ [2] | Second version of Standing Committee on Finance drafts ⚠️ [2] |
| Adaptation / GGA (Global Goal on Adaptation) | Draft: adaptation finance of "at least USD 120 billion" ✅ [14] | USD 120 billion baseline demanded by developing countries ⚠️ [3] | The Presidency will prepare a revised text ⚠️ [2] |
| Just transition | Contact group co-chaired by **Federica Fricano (Italy)** and **Joseph Teo (Singapore)** ⚠️ [2] | **China**: dedicated body; for the **EU** a "red line" ⚠️ [2] | Informal note ⚠️ [2] |
