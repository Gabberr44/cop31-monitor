# AI and climate information integrity: the priorities for action
**When:** 13/11 (time n.a.) · **Where:** Antalya EXPO Center · **Organiser:** Global Initiative for Information Integrity on Climate Change (UN, UNESCO, Brazil) ✅ [1]<br>
**Speakers:** **Charlotte Scaddan** (Information Integrity Senior Advisor, UN Department of Global Communications); **Jake Dubbins** (co-founder, Conscious Advertising Network) ✅ [1]<br>
🎥 Video not yet available

## At a glance
The initiative co-chaired by the UN, UNESCO and Brazil launched priorities for action on artificial intelligence (AI) and reliable climate information. It matters because it asks AI companies to disclose their energy use and environmental footprints: a transparency signal that will also reach business customers of digital services.

## Key points
- **The Global Initiative for Information Integrity on Climate Change** published the "Priorities for Action on AI and Climate Information Integrity" ✅ [1].
- **The priorities** ask AI companies to assess how their systems affect climate information and to publish the findings ✅ [1].
- **The priorities** also ask for disclosure of AI energy use and carbon, water and land footprints ✅ [1].
- **States** are invited to integrate information integrity into national AI strategies and climate plans ✅ [1].
- **The Conscious Advertising Network** launched a Charter for Climate Information Integrity in Advertising, with more transparency on the ad supply chain ✅ [1].

## Announcements and numbers
- **Germany** committed more than USD 2.2 million to research, communication strategies and public awareness campaigns ✅ [1].

## Quotes
- "Whether people can rely on what they're told by AI and about it." — **Charlotte Scaddan, UN Department of Global Communications** [1]

## Relevance for the VSME
**Medium** — No obligation for SMEs, but the call for data on AI energy use reinforces the trend of asking digital suppliers (cloud, data centres) for the emissions of their services: useful for estimating Scope 3, and for B3 (energy and GHG) if the SME runs its own servers.
