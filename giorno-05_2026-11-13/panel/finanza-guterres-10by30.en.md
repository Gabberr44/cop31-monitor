# Climate finance: Guterres's press conference and the "10by30" initiative
**When:** 13/11; Guterres's press conference at 16:30 Antalya (14:30 Italian time) according to the UNFCCC calendar ⚠️ (search results only) · **Where:** Antalya EXPO Center, press room · **Organiser:** Office of the UN Secretary-General; British International Investment, Norfund, FinDev Canada<br>
**Speakers:** **António Guterres** (UN Secretary-General); **Simon Stiell** (UNFCCC Executive Secretary)<br>
🎥 [Full video — António Guterres press conference (UN Web TV, 20:52)](https://webtv.un.org/en/asset/k1d/k1dgleivnu) [5]

## At a glance
On finance day, Guterres called developed countries' climate finance insufficient and three public development banks launched a USD 10 billion initiative. It matters because more finance for climate projects brings more climate data requests along supply chains.

## Key points
- **António Guterres, UN Secretary-General**, told journalists at his last COP press conference that even tripling adaptation finance would meet less than 30% of needs ✅ [1].
- **António Guterres** met the heads of international climate funds and asked for fewer bureaucratic barriers to accessing funds ✅ [1].
- **António Guterres** described climate finance as marked by "structural injustice" 🟡 [2][3]. According to him, one dollar of capital in development banks can generate 8–10 dollars of financing ✅ [1].
- **British International Investment (UK), Norfund (Norway) and FinDev Canada** launched an initiative to channel USD 10 billion into climate projects in emerging markets by 2030 ✅ [1][4].
- **The three institutions** will share investment pipelines, coordinate due diligence and cooperate on climate reporting ⚠️ according to Reuters [4]. Sectors: renewables, grids, storage, sustainable fuels, industrial decarbonisation ⚠️ according to Reuters [4].
- **Simon Stiell, UNFCCC Executive Secretary**, spoke at a ministerial meeting on debt and climate finance ✅ [1].

## Announcements and numbers
- **NCQG target** (the new collective quantified goal on climate finance): at least USD 300 billion a year from developed countries by 2035, within a broader USD 1.3 trillion goal ✅ [1].
- **According to UN News**, on current trends annual climate finance could fall USD 840 billion short of the broader goal ✅ [1].
- **Kaveh Zahedi, FAO**: only about 5% of climate-related development finance goes to agriculture ✅ [1].

## Quotes
- "Even tripling adaptation finance would meet less than 30 per cent of what is needed." — **António Guterres, UN Secretary-General** [1]
- "Climate finance must help countries break free." — **Simon Stiell, UNFCCC Executive Secretary** [1]

## Relevance for the VSME
**Medium** — Development banks that "cooperate on climate reporting" ask for data from the companies they finance and their suppliers; a completed VSME speeds up the answer.
