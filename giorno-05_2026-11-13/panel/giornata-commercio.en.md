# Trade day: climate, trade and competitiveness
**When:** 13/11, 08:00–20:00 Antalya (06:00–18:00 Italian time) · **Where:** Antalya EXPO Center, Specialized Event Zones 3 and 4 · **Organiser:** COP31 Presidency and Türkiye's Ministry of Trade, with UNCTAD, WTO and ITC ✅ [1][2]<br>
**High-level panel speakers (08:45–09:45 Antalya, 06:45–07:45 Italian time):** **Ömer Bolat** (Minister of Trade, Türkiye); **André Aranha Corrêa do Lago** (COP30 President, Brazil); **Jean-Marie Paugam** (Deputy Director-General, WTO); **John Denton** (Secretary General, International Chamber of Commerce – ICC); **Jaime de Bourbon de Parme** (Director of Environment, OECD); **Pedro Manuel Moreno** (Acting Secretary-General, UNCTAD) ✅ [1]<br>
🎥 Video not yet available

## At a glance
The Presidency devoted a full day to the relationship between climate rules and trade, including a session on SMEs. It matters for exporting SMEs because speakers discussed carbon data, traceability and common rules for measuring carbon.

## Key points
- **Ömer Bolat, Türkiye's Trade Minister**, said trade should support climate action, not hinder it ⚠️ according to Climate Home [3].
- **John Denton, ICC Secretary General**, warned that the number of trade measures makes operations difficult and costly ⚠️ according to Climate Home [3].
- **Jean-Marie Paugam, WTO Deputy Director-General**, called for more transparency and help for developing countries to meet trade rules ⚠️ according to Climate Home [3].
- **Abdul Awal Mintoo, Bangladesh's Environment Minister**, said the CBAM would disproportionately affect developing countries ⚠️ according to Climate Home [3].
- **Nine trade ministers of the Coalition of Trade Ministers on Climate**, including **Maroš Šefčovič, EU Trade Commissioner**, call for cooperation at the WTO on transparency and interoperability of climate measures 🟡 [4].
- **ITC (International Trade Centre)** led the session "Empowering SMEs for Climate-Ready Trade": access to knowledge, digital tools, finance and markets ✅ [1]. No written account found.

## Announcements and numbers
- **Session 4 (19:00–20:00 Antalya, 17:00–18:00 Italian time)**: interoperability on data, carbon accounting, MRV (measurement, reporting and verification) systems and traceability, with **ISO, OECD, WBCSD and ResponsibleSteel** ✅ [1].

## Quotes
- "Trade should be an enabler to climate action, not an additional barrier to it." — **Ömer Bolat, Türkiye's Trade Minister** [3]

## Relevance for the VSME
**High** — Climate data requests from foreign customers travel through trade: common carbon accounting rules would reduce the different questionnaires an SME receives, and the VSME (B3, energy and GHG emissions) is the basis for answering them.
