# Final decision: the Presidencies' outline and the Parties' reactions
**When:** 13/11, consultations during the day (times not published) · **Where:** Antalya EXPO Center, with overflow rooms and online platform · **Organiser:** COP31 Presidency (Türkiye) and Presidency of Negotiations (Australia) ✅ [1][2]<br>
**Co-facilitators:** **Ayşin Turpancı** (COP31 Presidency) and **Sally Box** (Presidency of Negotiations) for the cover decision; **Kushla Munro** (Presidency of Negotiations) and **Ayşin Turpancı** for strengthening the UNFCCC process 🟡 [1]<br>
🎥 No video foreseen (informal consultations)

## At a glance
The Presidencies circulated an informal note with possible elements of the "cover decision" (the COP's final political text) and collected the groups' reactions. It matters for business because the outline touches electrification, methane and trade measures such as the CBAM (the EU carbon border levy).

## Key points
- **The COP31 Presidencies** circulated a "summary of reflections": a menu of possible elements that does not reflect consensus ✅ [2][3].
- **According to Climate Home and Down To Earth**, the elements include: limiting the duration and size of the 1.5 °C overshoot, the "35-by-35" electrification goal, methane, the transition away from fossil fuels and a time-bound phase-out of unilateral trade measures ⚠️ [2][4].
- **The Arab Group** said top-down sectoral targets interfere with national priorities 🟡 [1]. It asked developed countries to phase out unilateral trade measures 🟡 [1].
- **The European Union** asked that the outcome create no new mandates 🟡 [1]. It wants to reference the duration and magnitude of the 1.5 °C overshoot and the transition away from fossil fuels 🟡 [1].
- **The African Group** opposed electrification targets without specifying the means of implementation 🟡 [1]. **Grupo Sur** asks not to impose additional reporting requirements on developing countries 🟡 [1].
- **Russia, the Arab Group and China** questioned the mandate for the consultations on reforming the UNFCCC process 🟡 [1]. **Kushla Munro, co-facilitator**, replied that all views will be heard before proposing a way forward 🟡 [1].

## Announcements and numbers
- **The United Kingdom** recalled its commitment to deliver GBP 6 billion in climate finance over the next three years 🟡 [1].
- **The Coalition for Rainforest Nations (CfRN)** urged governments to encourage companies to buy Article 6.2 credits (country-to-country traded reductions) 🟡 [1].

## Relevance for the VSME
**Medium** — No obligation for SMEs, but a paragraph on unilateral trade measures would increase political pressure on the CBAM, which requires embedded-emissions data from suppliers.
