## 🇬🇧 Post EN

Where early warning works, disaster deaths are 6.1 times lower. For an SME, physical risk is no longer a big-company topic.

🔙 Yesterday
• No reporting decisions, but three useful signals.
• UN early warning report: 136 countries covered, one in three still without. This is the public data to start from when assessing floods and extreme heat at your own sites.
• Guterres asked UNEP for a global "near-zero" methane standard for oil and gas. Businesses in the gas supply chain can expect sharper questions about leaks.
• No new public finance pledges from leaders. My reading: more room for private lenders, who ask for standard data.

▶️ Today (Rome time)
• 07:00–08:30 leaders' summit on greenhouse gases other than CO₂, led by the United Kingdom.
• 08:00–10:30 COP31 Business Forum, opening plenary with Türkiye's chambers of commerce (TOBB) and the Presidency.
• 11:30–15:00 dialogue on urban resilience and adaptation with UN-Habitat.

✅ Two actions for your SME
1. In the VSME (B3), Scope 1 also covers gas and refrigerant leaks: record your equipment's annual top-ups separately.
2. The Basic Module does not require a physical risk analysis, but banks and insurers do: note the main hazards for each site using public maps.

Has your bank or insurer already asked about the climate risks of your sites? Yes, no, or coming soon?
