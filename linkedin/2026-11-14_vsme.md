### Post text (English)
Every foreign customer asks for emissions in a different format. Yesterday at COP31 a session with the WTO, ISO and the OECD was devoted to exactly this.

🔙 Yesterday, through an SME's eyes
• The COP31 Presidency, with the WTO, UNCTAD and ITC, held a session on interoperability of data, carbon accounting and verification. ISO, the OECD and WBCSD took part too.
• ITC led another session on preparing SMEs for "climate-ready" trade.
• Nine trade ministers, including Maroš Šefčovič (EU), call for more transparency and less fragmented rules.
• The UN, UNESCO and Brazil ask artificial intelligence companies to disclose energy use and carbon footprint.

▶️ Today, from a reporting angle
• Countries present their climate transparency reports in a peer-review session.
• 14:00–15:00 (Rome time): UNIDO and UNEP FI on the market for adaptation solutions.

✅ Two actions for your SME
1. In VSME B3 (energy and GHG), record the emission factors and sources you used. The same data will serve several customers.
2. Ask your cloud or digital service provider for the energy use or emissions of the service you use.

How many different emissions questionnaires do you get each year from customers and banks?
