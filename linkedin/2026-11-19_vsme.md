### Post text (English)
75 countries back Türkiye's goal: electricity at 35% of energy use by 2035. Does your SME know how much it uses?

🔙 Yesterday, through an SME's eyes
• UN News counts 75 countries backing "35x35", the Turkish Presidency's electrification goal.
• Simon Stiell (UNFCCC) opened the second Global Stocktake. It will shape new national climate plans (NDCs): EU and national rules for companies follow from them.
• The ministers' exchange did not settle the knots. The 35% goal and the paragraph on "unilateral" trade measures, a category that includes CBAM, are still open in the draft.

▶️ Today, from a reporting perspective
• The Australian Presidency is preparing a shorter draft. To check: do electrification and trade measures survive?
• From 12:00 to 14:00 (Italian time), high-level event on global climate action: the track for voluntary initiatives, open to companies too.

✅ Two actions for your SME
1. In VSME B3 (energy and greenhouse gases), report total consumption in MWh, separating fuels and electricity and, if you can, the renewable share.
2. Pick a base year and keep the same calculation method: year-on-year comparison is the proof of progress banks and customers will ask for.

In your company, do you already know what share of your energy use is electricity?
