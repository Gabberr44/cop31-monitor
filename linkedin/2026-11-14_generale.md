### Post text (English)
Even tripling adaptation finance would cover less than 30% of needs. So said António Guterres, at his last COP as Secretary-General.

🔙 Yesterday ("Finance and Trade" day)
• António Guterres, UN Secretary-General, asked climate funds to cut red tape for accessing finance.
• The COP31 Presidencies (Türkiye and Australia) circulated the outline of the final decision: it includes 35% electricity by 2035, methane and finance.
• Chris Bowen, President of Negotiations, warned that the outline is not a negotiating text.
• The public development banks of the UK, Norway and Canada launched USD 10 billion for climate in emerging markets by 2030.

▶️ Today (Rome time)
• Thematic day: children, youth, education and skills.
• Afternoon: closing plenaries of the Convention's technical bodies. We will see what goes up to ministers.
• By tonight countries send their views on the final decision.
• 16:30–18:00: session on private investment in the transition, with UNEP FI.

On Sunday the venue is closed. The official draft is expected early next week.

Should a target like "35% electricity by 2035" make it into the final decision?
