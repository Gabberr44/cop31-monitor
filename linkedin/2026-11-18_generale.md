### Post text (English)
"Many of the characteristics of a shopping list." That is how the EU dismissed COP31's first draft deal.

🔙 Yesterday ("Ocean, Seas, Nature and Land Use" day)
• Wopke Hoekstra, EU Climate Commissioner, said the draft "needs significant change". He wants the strongest possible signal on clean energy.
• Chris Bowen, COP31 President of Negotiations, admitted not all Parties agree. He promised at least one more draft by Friday.
• Ilana Seid, chair of the small island states (AOSIS), called any delay to IPCC reports "unjustifiable". Saudi Arabia denies trying to slow down science.
• UN News: more than 95% of coastal and island countries include the ocean in their climate plans.

▶️ Today ("Human and Social Development and Gender" day, Rome time)
• 8:00–12:00: last ministers' statements. Among others Portugal, India, Sweden, the Netherlands, France and Russia.
• From 13:00: observer organisations speak, including business and trade unions.
• The Australian Presidency hears countries' reactions and looks for compromises for the new draft.

Between now and Friday we will see how much of the draft is left standing.
