🔙 Sabato, letto con gli occhi di una PMI

• GRI modera un panel UNFCCC sull'azione climatica di imprese ed enti locali.

✅ Due azioni per la tua PMI
1. Nel B3 del VSME (energia e gas serra) separa i consumi di elettricità da quelli di combustibili. Indica la quota rinnovabile se la conosci.
2. Chiedi al fornitore di energia il mix della tua fornitura o le garanzie d'origine.

Sapete già quanta della vostra energia arriva dall'elettricità e quanta da gas o gasolio?

### Post text (English)
Countries could not even agree on how to publish their own emissions data. The topic returns in June 2027.

🔙 Saturday, through an SME's eyes
• Adonia Ayebare, chair of the UN climate convention's scientific body (SBSTA), recorded the deadlock on the online tool for national greenhouse gas data. Nothing changes for companies' emission factors.
• The least developed countries and Ukraine flagged methodologies that are hard to apply in the new UN carbon credit market (Article 6.4), especially for small projects.
• UNEP FI had scheduled a session to speed up banks' and insurers' transition plans. Those plans also rely on client data.

▶️ Today, from a reporting angle
• "Science, Industry and Technology" day.
• 13:30–14:30 (Rome time): UNIDO on industrial electrification.
• GRI moderates a UNFCCC panel on climate action by companies and local governments.

✅ Two actions for your SME
1. In VSME B3 (energy and GHG emissions) separate electricity consumption from fuels. State the renewable share if you know it.
2. Ask your energy supplier for your supply mix or guarantees of origin.

Do you already know how much of your energy comes from electricity and how much from gas or diesel?
