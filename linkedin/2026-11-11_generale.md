## 🇮🇹 Post IT

Elettricità al 35% dei consumi entro il 2035: la COP31 ha il suo obiettivo simbolo. Ma nel testo manca la parola "pulita".

🔙 Ieri (giornata dell'energia)
• La Presidenza turca ha lanciato il pacchetto "35-by-35": portare l'elettricità dal 23% al 35% dei consumi finali, con un sistema di monitoraggio affidato all'IEA. L'adesione è volontaria.
• Il punto critico: l'obiettivo non richiede che la nuova elettricità arrivi da fonti pulite. Alcuni produttori di fossili si opporranno a ogni riferimento nella decisione finale.

## 🇬🇧 Post EN

Electricity at 35% of energy use by 2035: COP31 has its flagship goal. But the word "clean" is missing from the text.

🔙 Yesterday (energy day)
• The Turkish Presidency launched the "35-by-35" package: raising electricity from 23% to 35% of final energy consumption, with tracking entrusted to the IEA. Participation is voluntary.
• The sticking point: the goal does not require new electricity to come from clean sources. Some fossil fuel producers will resist any such reference in the final decision.
• The COP30 Presidency presented Brazil's fossil fuel roadmap in plenary: national plans by 2028, supply cuts from 2031. Guterres called for an end to treating the transition away from fossil fuels as taboo. It stays outside the formal negotiations.
• Global Carbon Budget: fossil CO₂ emissions −0.4% in 2026, but less than four years of 1.5 °C budget remain.

▶️ Today (Rome time): Leaders' Summit, day one
• 10:15 opening with Erdoğan, Guterres and the Australian Prime Minister.
• From 12:30 heads of state speak; expected speakers include Lula, von der Leyen and Han Zheng. The United States is absent for the second year running.
• 13:00 high-level dialogue on "35 by 35".

From here to Friday 20 the question is one: will 35% make it into the final decision, and in what words?

Does an electricity goal without the word "clean" help the transition or slow it down? Tell me why in the comments.
