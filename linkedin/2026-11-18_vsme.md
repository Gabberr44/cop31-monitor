• Giornata dedicata a sviluppo umano, sociale e genere.

✅ Due azioni per la tua PMI
1. Se importi acciaio, alluminio, cemento o fertilizzanti, continua a chiedere ai fornitori le emissioni incorporate. Il CBAM resta in vigore: una decisione COP non lo abolisce.
2. Nel B8 del VSME (forza lavoro) prepara i dati sui dipendenti per genere e tipo di contratto.

### Post text (English)
Paragraph 119: the COP31 draft asks rich countries to phase out "unilateral" climate trade measures by 2027.

🔙 Yesterday, through an SME's eyes
• The COP31 Presidency published the official draft of the final decision. CBAM, the EU mechanism that puts a carbon price on certain imported goods, is not named.
• The same draft calls for raising electricity to 35% of final energy consumption by 2035. The EU backs the goal.
• Germanwatch presented the Climate Risk Index 2027: more than 916,000 deaths from extreme events from 1996 to 2025.

▶️ Today, from a reporting perspective
• The draft is not agreed: a new version is expected by Friday.
• From 13:00 (Rome time) observers take the floor, among them business organisations (BINGO).
• A day devoted to human and social development and gender.

✅ Two actions for your SME
1. If you import steel, aluminium, cement or fertilisers, keep asking suppliers for embedded emissions. CBAM stays in force: a COP decision does not abolish it.
2. In VSME B8 (workforce), prepare employee data by gender and contract type.

In your company, who collects emissions data from foreign suppliers?
