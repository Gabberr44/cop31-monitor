# COP31 — Day 7 · Monday 16 November 2026
🕘 Provisional evening version — to be consolidated tomorrow morning at 09:30 (Italian time)<br>
First draft: 16/11/2026 at 22:45 (Italian time).

Theme of the day chosen by the **COP31 Presidency (Türkiye)**: "Science, Industry and Technology" ✅ [1]. It is the first day of the second and final week: the conference closes on Friday 20 November 🟡 [4][9].

## At a glance
- **What happened:** **Chris Bowen, COP31 President of Negotiations (Australia)**, published the first draft of the "cover decision", the conference's final political text 🟡 [3][4]. **The COP31 Presidency and the ITU (the UN digital agency)** launched the Antalya Pledge on artificial intelligence ✅ [5][6].
- **Why it matters:** the draft puts everything on the table, from exiting fossil fuels to $120 billion for adaptation; many countries are already contesting its content 🟡 [2][4].
- **Tomorrow:** on Tuesday 17 the high-level segment with ministers opens ✅ [5].

## Main facts
1. **Chris Bowen, COP31 President of Negotiations (Australia)**, released an 18-page first draft of the final decision 🟡 [3][4]. The text gathers options from 45 written submissions representing more than 160 governments ⚠️ according to Climate Home [3]. **Bowen** said: "It won't be the final draft" 🟡 [4]. He added that the text will be narrowed to issues where there is common ground 🟡 [3][4].
2. **The Presidency's draft** calls for "accelerated action to phase out fossil fuels" ⚠️ according to AFP [4]. It asks rich countries to reach net zero emissions by 2030 🟡 [3][4]. It proposes a goal of 35% electricity in final energy consumption by 2035 🟡 [3][4]. It specifies at least $120 billion a year for adaptation by 2035 🟡 [3][4]. It expresses "deep concern" over the impending breach of 1.5 °C ⚠️ according to AFP [4].
3. **The Like-Minded Developing Countries (LMDCs, including China, India and Saudi Arabia)** said the draft crosses their "red lines" ⚠️ according to ENB [2]. They contest the 35% electrification goal and the roadmap on transitioning away from fossil fuels ⚠️ [2]. **The Arab Group and the Russian Federation** rejected the draft ⚠️ [2]. **The United Kingdom** instead wants a strong response to the 1.5 °C overshoot ⚠️ [2].
4. **The COP31 Presidency and the International Telecommunication Union (ITU)** launched the Antalya Pledge on artificial intelligence (AI) ✅ [6]. It is voluntary and has four pillars, including measuring and reporting AI's environmental footprint ✅ [6]. Progress will be reviewed every year at the COP until 2030 ✅ [6].<br>
   🎥 [Full video — High-level Roundtable on AI (UNFCCC Global Climate Action YouTube channel)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5sDPFX--RVY)
5. **Tomas Lamanauskas, ITU Deputy Secretary-General**, said data-centre energy use for AI will double by 2030 ✅ [5]. According to the ITU, by 2030 AI data centres could use nearly 945 TWh of electricity a year ✅ [11]. According to UN News the Pledge had 80 endorsements and 271 commitments on Monday morning ✅ [5]. **Climate Home** instead counts nine countries and over 40 companies as signatories ⚠️ [3].
6. **Several countries, from the LMDCs to the Least Developed Countries**, are asking to revisit the integrity rules of the UN carbon crediting mechanism (Art. 6.4) 🟡 [2][8]. The draft asks to avoid excessive "conservativeness" in calculating credits 🟡 [2][8]. **The European Union, Switzerland and Senegal** oppose this 🟡 [2][8].
7. **Laurence Tubiana, CEO of the European Climate Foundation**, told the science plenary: "We have to defend science" ✅ [5]. **Mary Robinson, former President of Ireland**, urged not to undermine the IPCC (the UN climate science panel) ✅ [5]. **Solomon Islands** is the first country to announce a contribution to the Green Climate Fund at COP31 ✅ [5].

## Negotiations: where things stand
| Topic | What moved | What is blocked | Texts released |
|---|---|---|---|
| Final decision ("cover decision") | **Presidency**: first 18-page draft; consultations led by **Ayşin Turpancı (COP31 Presidency)** and **Sally Box (Presidency of Negotiations)** 🟡 [2][4] | **LMDCs, Arab Group, Russia** oppose sectoral targets and the 35% electricity goal ⚠️ [2]; **Azerbaijan**: draft inconsistent with the UN process ⚠️ [2] | Presidency draft 🟡 [3][4] |
| Mitigation and science | Draft: IPCC reports ready before the 2028 Global Stocktake (the Paris Agreement's global review) 🟡 [3][4] | **Saudi Arabia and India** want IPCC reports delayed to 2029 ⚠️ according to AFP [4] | Presidency draft 🟡 [3][4] |
| Adaptation / GGA (Global Goal on Adaptation) | **G77/China** (developing countries): triple adaptation finance, with a baseline and tracking method ⚠️ [2] | **AILAC (an alliance of Latin American countries), AOSIS (the small island states) and the EU** object to options for "different warming increments" ⚠️ [2] | Draft text ⚠️ [2] |
| Finance | The figure of $120 billion a year for adaptation by 2035 keeps recurring 🟡 [2][3][4] | **Art. 2.1(c)** (aligning finance flows): **G77/China** refuses to work on the co-facilitators' text ⚠️ [2] | Revised drafts on long-term finance, GEF (the Global Environment Facility), Adaptation Fund ⚠️ [2] |
| Article 6 (carbon markets) | **Art. 6.4**: revised decision text 🟡 [2][8] | **EU**: no to "avoiding over-conservativeness", methodologies must stay rigorous 🟡 [2][8] | Draft CMA decision (the Paris Agreement assembly) 🟡 [2][8] |
| Just transition | Contact group co-chaired by **Federica Fricano (Italy)** and **Joseph Teo (Singapore)** ⚠️ [2]; the proposed mechanism has a policy arm and an implementation arm ⚠️ [10] | **Arab Group**: no references to carbon pricing and leakage measures ⚠️ [2] | Informal note ⚠️ [2] |
| Trade / unilateral measures | Draft: developed countries should phase out "unilateral" trade measures ⚠️ according to Climate Home [3] | **Arab Group and Nigeria**: negative impacts of unilateral trade measures ⚠️ [2] | Presidency draft ⚠️ [3] |
