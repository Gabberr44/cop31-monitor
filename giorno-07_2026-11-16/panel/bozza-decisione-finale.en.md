# The first draft of the final decision ("cover decision")
**When:** 16/11, afternoon and evening Antalya time (Italian time: −2 h) · **Where:** Presidency consultations, Antalya EXPO Center · **Organiser:** COP31 Presidency (Türkiye) and Presidency of Negotiations (Australia)<br>
**Speakers:** **Chris Bowen** (COP31 President of Negotiations, Australian Minister for Climate Change and Energy); **Ayşin Turpancı** (co-facilitator, COP31 Presidency); **Sally Box** (co-facilitator, Presidency of Negotiations)<br>
🎥 No video expected (closed consultations)

## At a glance
The two Presidencies published an 18-page first draft of COP31's final political text. It contains almost every country's demand, including those many reject: ministers now have to choose what to keep.

## Key points
- **Chris Bowen, COP31 President of Negotiations**, published the draft and said it will be narrowed to issues with common ground 🟡 [1][2].
- **The draft** asks rich countries for net zero emissions by 2030 and proposes 35% electricity in final energy consumption by 2035 🟡 [1][2].
- **The draft** specifies at least $120 billion a year for adaptation by 2035 🟡 [1][2].
- **The draft** asks for IPCC (the UN climate science panel) reports to be ready before the 2028 Global Stocktake 🟡 [1][2]. **Saudi Arabia and India** want them delayed to 2029 ⚠️ according to AFP [2].
- **The Like-Minded Developing Countries (LMDCs), the Arab Group and Russia** rejected sectoral targets and the 35% target ⚠️ according to ENB [3]. **The Environmental Integrity Group (EIG, including Switzerland and South Korea)** asks for balanced language ⚠️ [3].
- **Heike Henn, State Secretary at the German environment ministry**, said neither the fossil fuel roadmap nor the electrification goal necessarily needs to be in the decision ⚠️ according to Climate Home [1].

## Announcements and numbers
- 18 pages; 45 written submissions from more than 160 governments ⚠️ according to Climate Home [1].
- 144 paragraphs to coordinate "in one hour", according to a negotiator quoted by ENB ⚠️ [3].

## Quotes
- "There are elements in this draft that not all parties will agree on. That is a given." — **Chris Bowen, COP31 President of Negotiations** [2]
- "We will need to find some kind of balance." — **Jean-Luc Crucke, Belgian Climate Minister** [2]

## Relevance for the VSME
**Medium** — A global goal on electrification and net zero would steer EU policies and therefore the energy data requests (VSME B3) to SMEs; for now it is only a draft.
