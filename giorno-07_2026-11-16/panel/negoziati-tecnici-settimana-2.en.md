# Technical negotiations: adaptation, finance, just transition
**When:** 16/11, all day · **Where:** informal consultations and contact groups, Antalya EXPO Center · **Organiser:** UNFCCC<br>
**Speakers:** **Cornelia Jäger** (Austria, GGA: the Global Goal on Adaptation); **Georg Børsting** (Norway) and **Elchin Alakbarov** (Azerbaijan), climate finance work programme; **Federica Fricano** (Italy) and **Joseph Teo** (Singapore), just transition; **Ali Mohammed** (Kenya) and **Mattias Frumerie** (Sweden), Art. 2.1(c)<br>
🎥 No video expected (informal consultations)

## At a glance
Before handing over to ministers, negotiators reopened every technical text. The common knot is finance: who pays, how much and how it is measured.

## Key points
- **The G77/China (developing countries)** asked to triple adaptation finance and to define its baseline, method and tracking right away ⚠️ according to ENB [1]. **The EU, the EIG (Environmental Integrity Group, including Switzerland and South Korea) and Japan** want this discussed in the finance negotiations ⚠️ [1].
- **In the corridors** the figure circulating is $120 billion a year, three times the $40 billion of the Glasgow Pact 🟡 [1][2].
- **On Art. 2.1(c) (aligning finance flows with climate goals)**, the **G77/China** refused to work on the co-facilitators' text; **the EU, New Zealand, Canada, Switzerland and the UK** expressed disappointment ⚠️ [1].
- **On just transition**, the **Arab Group** objected to references to carbon pricing and carbon leakage measures; **the EU** called for stronger labour rights ⚠️ [1].
- **The African Group, the LDCs and AOSIS (the small island states)** want the just transition mechanism operational immediately; **the EIG** prefers a phased approach ⚠️ [1].
- **The Arab Group, Nigeria and Indonesia** want a mechanism without new reporting obligations ⚠️ [1].

## Relevance for the VSME
**Low** — Art. 2.1(c) concerns finance flows and could in future push banks to ask clients for more climate data; today the negotiation is stalled.
