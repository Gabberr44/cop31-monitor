# Science, industry and technology
**When:** 16/11, thematic day (times n/a) · **Where:** Antalya EXPO Center · **Organiser:** COP31 Presidency<br>
**Speakers:** **Laurence Tubiana** (CEO, European Climate Foundation); **Mary Robinson** (former President of Ireland); **Ciyong Zou** (Deputy Director-General, UNIDO); **Michael Apicelli** (head of the Climate Club Secretariat); **Peter Schniering** (founder, Future Cleantech Architects)<br>

## At a glance
The thematic day combined defending climate science, under pressure in the negotiations, and a push for green industrialisation. For industry the message is that technologies exist: investment is missing.

## Key points
- **Laurence Tubiana, CEO of the European Climate Foundation**, told the science plenary: "We have to defend science. We have to defend the scientists" ✅ [1].
- **Mary Robinson, former President of Ireland**, urged on-time delivery of IPCC (the UN climate science panel) reports ✅ [1]. **Vulnerable countries** want the reports in 2028, before the second Global Stocktake (the Paris Agreement's global review) ✅ [1].
- **Several governments** launched the Antalya Green Industrialization Compact at a ministerial meeting: investment, markets for cleaner industrial products, access to technology ✅ [1]. The exact launch date is unclear: a trade outlet already wrote about it on 11/11 ⚠️.
- **Michael Apicelli, head of the Climate Club Secretariat (53 member countries)**, noted that steel and cement investments lock in technologies for 30–40 years ✅ [1].
- **A UNESCO–UCL study** found that chatbots sometimes abandon established climate facts when challenged ✅ [1].

## Quotes
- "The world cannot afford to undermine or lose the IPCC." — **Mary Robinson, former President of Ireland** [1]
- "The technologies are here today. The investments just need to be made." — **Michael Apicelli, Climate Club** [1]

## Relevance for the VSME
**Medium** — SME suppliers in steel, cement and manufacturing will receive more and more requests for product carbon-intensity data; the VSME (B3, energy and greenhouse gases) is the basis for answering.
