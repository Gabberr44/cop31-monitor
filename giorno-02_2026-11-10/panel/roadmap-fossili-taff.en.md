# Transition away from fossil fuels (TAFF) roadmap: the COP30 Presidency presents its toolkit
**When:** 10/11, time n/a · **Where:** Antalya EXPO Center, in plenary · **Organiser:** COP30 Presidency (Brazil) [1][3][5]<br>
**Speakers:** **André Corrêa do Lago** (President, COP30) [5]; **António Guterres** (Secretary-General, UN) [1][2][5][6]. According to Climate Home, **India**, **Nigeria**, **Germany**, the **African Group** (with **Antwi-Boasiako Amoah**, Ghana), **Laurent Fabius** and **John Kerry** also spoke [5]<br>
🎥 [Video integrale — COP30 Presidency event on the fossil fuel transition roadmap (UN Web TV, 1:51:29)](https://webtv.un.org/en/asset/k1b/k1brkp0hqo)

✅ Final version — consolidated on 11/11/2026 at 07:45 (Italian time)

## In brief
**The COP30 Presidency (Brazil)** presented in Antalya a toolkit to plan the TAFF (transition away from fossil fuels), after publishing its roadmap on its own on 3 Nov [2][4]. It matters because at Belém (COP30) there was no agreement on who should draft it, and COP31 has no fossil fuel agenda item [2][4].

## Key points
- **The COP30 Presidency** presented a toolkit to help countries plan the transition away from coal, oil and gas 🟡 [1][2].
- **António Guterres, UN Secretary-General**, called for national roadmaps with timelines, responsibilities and financing needs 🟡 [1]. According to Climate Home, he said that by the 2028 Global Stocktake (the global review of the Paris Agreement) "every country should be able to show what it has done to cut fossil fuel use or production" 🟡 [1]. According to ABC, he urged Türkiye and Australia to take the roadmap forward 🟡 [2].
- **Brazil** frames the roadmap as a call to action, not a text for negotiation 🟡 [3][4][5]. **Some fossil fuel producing countries** are expected to resist anchoring it in the final decision 🟡 [1][5].
- **The roadmap** sets a timeline: countries plan and assess their energy systems by 2028 🟡 [5][6]. Cuts to fossil fuel supply start from 2031 🟡 [5][6].
- **India, Nigeria and Germany** voiced support, according to Climate Home ⚠️ [5]. **The Netherlands, the UK and Australia** had already done so, reported by a single source ⚠️ [5]. **The African Group** asks for flexibility on timelines and for finance and technology support [5].
- **Recep Tayyip Erdoğan, President of Türkiye**, celebrated on X on the same day the production increase at the new Gabar oilfields, according to Climate Home ⚠️ [1]. Output would exceed 100,000 barrels a day in 2027, reported by a single source ⚠️ [1].

## Announcements and numbers
- **The Powering Past Coal Alliance (an alliance for the coal phase-out)** launched "Coal Transition Partnerships", according to Climate Home ⚠️ [1]. The aim is to unlock over USD 23 bn for first coal phase-out plans in developing countries, reported by a single source ⚠️ [1].
- **No country** made binding commitments.

## Quotes
- "It is long past time to stop treating the transition away from fossil fuels as taboo or intractable." — **António Guterres, UN Secretary-General**, according to Reuters and Climate Home [5][6]
- "We don't need more promises, we need plans." — **John Kerry**, according to AP [7]

## Relevance for the VSME
**Low** — no direct effect, but if national roadmaps get deadlines, banks will change how they assess transition risk for companies most dependent on gas and diesel.
