# COP31 — Day 11 · Friday 20 November 2026
🕘 Provisional evening version — will be consolidated tomorrow morning at 09:30 (Italian time)<br>
⏱️ COP31 is in overtime: negotiations on the final decision continue overnight and on Saturday 21/11 🟡 [4][5]. Written on 20/11/2026 at 22:49 (Italian time).

Theme of the day chosen by the **COP31 Presidency (Türkiye)**: "Final Negotiations" ✅ [1].

## In brief
- **What happened:** on the last scheduled day **the COP31 Presidency** did not publish a new draft ✅ [3]. **Two coalitions of countries** want the transition away from fossil fuels back in the text ✅ [3] 🟡 [6][11].
- **Why it matters:** without consensus no decision passes; a single country, such as **Panama**, can block the final deal ✅ [3].
- **Tomorrow:** on Saturday 21 a third draft of the final decision and a new plenary are expected ⚠️ according to AFP [5].

## Key facts
1. **The COP31 Presidency** had not published a new version of the "Antalya Accord" (the final decision, or "cover decision") by Friday afternoon ✅ [3]. Delegations are still working on the draft of Thursday 19/11 ✅ [2][3].<br>
   🎥 Video not yet available
2. **The conference** went into overtime: negotiators from nearly 200 countries have not resolved their divisions on fossil fuels and finance 🟡 [4][5]. A closing session convened late in the evening approved only some technical items ⚠️ according to AFP [5].
3. **Murat Kurum, COP31 President and Turkish environment minister**, told the plenary: "We are close to the finish line, but we are not there yet" ⚠️ according to AFP [5]. **Chris Bowen, Australian climate minister and President of Negotiations**, will keep working "for as long as it takes" ⚠️ according to AFP [5].
4. **Nine countries led by France and Panama** (with Belgium, Côte d'Ivoire, Finland, Greece, Guatemala, Spain, Sweden and Vanuatu) said the draft is "not good enough" ✅ [3]. They ask for six priorities, including a "clear and orderly" transition away from fossil fuels ✅ [3].
5. **Ministers and envoys from 23 countries**, led by **Stientje van Veldhoven, Dutch climate minister**, want the "transition away from fossil fuels" put back in the text ⚠️ according to Carbon Brief [6]. They include Germany, the UK, Brazil, Indonesia, Norway and Pacific countries ⚠️ [6].
6. **China and Australia** signed a joint statement on climate change and the clean energy transition ✅ [8]. They welcome "on a voluntary basis" the goal of 35% electricity in final energy consumption by 2035 ✅ [8]. For **Carbon Brief** it is China's clearest endorsement yet of the "35 by 35" goal ⚠️ [7].
7. **The European Union** warns that the draft "risks turning this climate COP into a fossil summit" ⚠️ according to AFP [5]. **Climate Action Network (CAN, an international NGO network)** urged Global South countries to reject the text ✅ [3]. **The United States** is absent for the second year in a row ⚠️ according to Reuters [4][12].
