# Civil society rejects COP31
**When:** 20/11, afternoon, time n.a. · **Where:** Antalya EXPO Center, press room · **Organiser:** Climate Action Network (CAN) International<br>
**Speakers:** **Tasneem Essop** (Executive Director, CAN International); **Mohamed Adow** (Director, Power Shift Africa); **Anabella Rosemberg** (CAN); **Brandon Wu** (CAN)<br>
🎥 Video not yet available (re-checked on 21/11/2026)

## In brief
The main NGO networks closed COP31 with a very negative verdict and urged Global South countries to reject the text ✅ [1]. It matters because it measures the gap between the Presidency and the demands of the most vulnerable countries.

## Key points
- **Tasneem Essop, Executive Director of CAN International**, said the draft offers nothing to protect people or address Global South priorities ✅ [1].
- **Mohamed Adow, Director of Power Shift Africa**, called it "the worst COP ever" ✅ [1] 🟡 [2].
- **Activists** held black "shame" signs at CAN's final press conference ✅ [1].
- **Brandon Wu, of CAN**, said developed countries "came with nothing" on just transition, finance and adaptation ⚠️ according to Carbon Brief [3].
- **Sinéad Magner, of the Women and Gender Constituency**, accuses Norway, the UK, Canada, Japan, Australia and Türkiye of blocking progress ⚠️ according to Carbon Brief [3].

## Quotes
- "The worst COP ever." — **Mohamed Adow, Director of Power Shift Africa** [1]
