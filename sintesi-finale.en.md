# COP31 Antalya — final summary (9–22 November 2026)
✅ Written on 23/11/2026 at 09:56 (Italian time), after the conference closed<br>
Italian version: [sintesi-finale.md](sintesi-finale.md) · Daily summaries: [index](README.md) · Guide: [how the COP works](guida/come-funziona-la-cop.en.md)

## In brief
- **What happened:** **COP31 (the 31st UN climate conference)** closed on Sunday 22/11, two days late, without adopting the final political decision, the "Antalya Pact" ✅ [1][2].
- **Why it matters:** it is the first COP since 2009 without a common political text; some technical decisions and many voluntary initiatives remain, such as 35% electrification by 2035 ✅ [1][5].
- **For companies:** no new obligation and no obligation removed; in Europe data requests come from CSRD, VSME, CBAM and banks, not from the COP ✅ [11][12].

## How it went, in five steps
1. **The Turkish Presidency (Murat Kurum)** focused on a voluntary Action Agenda: "35 by 35" electrification, zero waste, ocean, artificial intelligence ✅ [5][6].
2. **Chris Bowen, Australian minister and President of Negotiations**, published three drafts of the final decision between 16 and 22/11 🟡 [3][7].
3. **The first draft** mentioned fossil fuels six times ⚠️ according to NBC News [4]. **The second draft** (19/11) removed them entirely ✅ [7].
4. **A front of more than 100 countries, led by the European Union**, asked to restore the transition away from fossil fuels; **Saudi Arabia and Russia** opposed it 🟡 [10][18].
5. **The 22/11 plenary** found no consensus: formal objection from the EU and Panama, no from the Marshall Islands ✅ [1].<br>
   🎥 [Full video — closing plenary (official UN Climate Change channel)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y3-yVRkgYBM)

## Main announcements (voluntary initiatives)
- **The COP31 Presidency** launched the "35 by 35" pledge on 10/11: electricity at 35% of final energy consumption by 2035 ✅ [5].
- **The COP31 Presidency and the ITU (the UN digital agency)** launched the Antalya AI Pledge on 16/11, calling for transparency on AI's energy use and environmental footprint ✅ [6].
- **Simon Stiell, UNFCCC Executive Secretary**, said around half the global economy is accelerating electrification thanks to country pledges ⚠️ according to Xinhua [16].
- **China and Australia** jointly backed the 35% goal on 20/11 🟡 (see [Day 11](giorno-11_2026-11-20/sintesi-giornata.en.md)).

## What changed vs. expectations
- **Expected:** a text following up on the fossil fuel roadmap announced in Belém (COP30). **Outcome:** no words on fossil fuels in the last drafts and no text adopted ✅ [1][3].
- **Expected:** a figure for tripling adaptation finance. **Outcome:** no figure; **António Guterres, UN Secretary-General**, asks for "dates and dollars" ✅ [2].
- **Expected:** an "implementation COP" driven by voluntary initiatives. **Outcome:** the initiatives exist, but without anchoring in UN decisions ✅ [1][5].
- **Unexpected:** the longest conference ever, beyond COP25 in Madrid (2019) ✅ [1] 🟡 [4][8].

## What it means for a European SME
- **No new obligation from COP31.** EU rules stand: Omnibus I (new CSRD scope, to be transposed by 19/03/2027) and CBAM (first annual declaration by 30/09/2027) ✅ [11][12].
- **The VSME (EFRAG's voluntary standard for SMEs)** remains the tool to answer customers and banks. The most requested data: energy by source and greenhouse gases (B3), and for Comprehensive Module users climate risks (C4).
- **The 1.5 °C overshoot** is now acknowledged by the UN ✅ [2]: expect more questions on the physical risks of your sites.
- **The electricity share of consumption** becomes a global indicator, albeit voluntary ✅ [5]: in B3 split electricity and fuels, in MWh.
