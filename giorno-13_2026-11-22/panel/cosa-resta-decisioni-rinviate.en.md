# What remains: decisions adopted and issues deferred
**When:** 22/11, written update during the closing plenary · **Where:** Carbon Brief Connect (online forum) · **Organiser:** Carbon Brief<br>
**Speakers:** **Simon Evans** (Carbon Brief); **Catherine Abreu** (International Climate Politics Hub); **Paul Watkinson** (former EU negotiator); **Joanna Depledge** (University of Cambridge); **Farhana Yamin** (climate lawyer); **Meena Raman** (Third World Network); **Alden Meyer** (E3G)<br>
🎥 Not applicable (written text)

## In brief
**Carbon Brief and six experts** explain that COP31 lost its political decision but adopted several technical decisions ⚠️ [1]. It matters because issues without agreement do not disappear: they move to Bonn in 2027 and to COP32 in Addis Ababa ⚠️ [1][2].

## Key points
- **Alden Meyer, E3G (climate think tank)**, lists among the decisions adopted the just transition work programme and the global goal on adaptation (GGA) ⚠️ [1]. **Simon Stiell, UNFCCC**, confirms the just transition agreement ✅ [2].
- **Paul Watkinson, former EU negotiator at COP21 in Paris**, sees the just transition mechanism as "weakened" ⚠️ [1].
- **Simon Evans, Carbon Brief**, reports that the mitigation work programme, "response measures" (impacts of climate policies on other countries) and ACE (climate education and participation) were rejected and return to Bonn in June 2027 ⚠️ [1].
- **Alden Meyer** attributes the block on the mitigation work programme to **Russia and Saudi Arabia** ⚠️ [1].
- **Catherine Abreu, International Climate Politics Hub**, notes that a political decision is not required to close a COP: many COPs had none ⚠️ [1]. **Joanna Depledge, University of Cambridge**, therefore does not call it a failure ⚠️ [1].
- **Meena Raman, Third World Network**, observes that developing countries' priorities (adaptation, just transition, finance) slip to COP32 ⚠️ [1].

## Announcements and numbers
- Precedents cited: COP6 in The Hague (2000), suspended and resumed the following year, and COP15 in Copenhagen (2009) ⚠️ [1].
- **COP32** will be held in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia ✅ [2].

## Relevance for the VSME
**Medium** — adopting the just transition decision and deferring mitigation create no obligations for SMEs, but confirm that data requests will come from the EU, banks and customers rather than from new UN rules.
