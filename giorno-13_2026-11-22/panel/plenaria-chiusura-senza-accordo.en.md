# Closing plenary: the Antalya Pact fails
**When:** 22/11, afternoon, Antalya (2 hours earlier in Italian time); exact time not given by official sources · **Where:** Antalya EXPO Center, plenary hall · **Organiser:** COP31 Presidencies (Türkiye and Australia)<br>
**Speakers:** **Murat Kurum** (COP31 President, Turkish Environment Minister); **Darragh O'Brien** (Irish Climate Minister, for the EU); delegations of Panama, the Marshall Islands, Ghana (African Group), Saudi Arabia, Pakistan<br>
🎥 [Full video — COP31 closing plenary (official UN Climate Change channel, 1 h 07')](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y3-yVRkgYBM)

## In brief
**The closing plenary** did not adopt COP31's final decision, the so-called "Antalya Pact", for lack of consensus ✅ [1][5]. It matters because the text was the political summary of the conference: without it COP31 leaves no new common commitments on fossil fuels and finance 🟡 [2][3].

## Key points
- **Murat Kurum, COP31 President**, said there was no agreement on the text and the decisions were not adopted ✅ [1]. He had earlier announced there would be no new draft ⚠️ according to The Independent [3].
- **The European Union** lodged a formal objection because the text did not mention fossil fuels ✅ [1].
- **The government of Panama** rejected the text and **the Marshall Islands** withheld support ✅ [1]. **Norway** and the group of **Least Developed Countries (44 countries)** also objected 🟡 [2][6].
- **Juan Carlos Navarro, Panama's Environment Minister**, said the world is "being held hostage by the petroleum mafia" ⚠️ according to NBC News [7].
- **Ghana, on behalf of the African Group (54 countries)**, did not block consensus, while criticising the text on finance and loss and damage ✅ [1].
- **Saudi Arabia** defended the references to carbon capture and storage and pledged to stay engaged ✅ [1]. **Pakistan** urged continued work towards consensus ✅ [1].
- **The last draft (version of 22/11, 02:00 Antalya)** contained none of the words "fossil", "oil" or "coal": it only recalled the COP28 "UAE Consensus" ✅ [8]. Details are in the [Day 12 panel](../../giorno-12_2026-11-21/panel/terza-bozza-decisione-finale.en.md).

## Announcements and numbers
- More than 44 hours past the scheduled close: the longest COP ever, beyond COP25 in Madrid (2019) 🟡 [2][3].
- According to CNN, more than 100 countries wanted to keep a reference to fossil fuels ⚠️ [2].

## Quotes
- "There are parties in this hall who oppose this text." — **Murat Kurum, COP31 President** [2]
- "It is unconscionable that we would have a climate pact that makes no mention of fossil fuels." — **Darragh O'Brien, Irish Climate Minister, on behalf of the EU** [7]

## Relevance for the VSME
**Medium** — no new or cancelled obligation for SMEs: EU rules (CSRD after the Omnibus, CBAM) remain, but there is no global signal pushing non-EU countries to align.
