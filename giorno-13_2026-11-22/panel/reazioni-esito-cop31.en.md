# Reactions from the UN, Presidencies and civil society
**When:** 22/11, during and after the closing plenary, Antalya (2 hours earlier in Italian time) · **Where:** Antalya EXPO Center and written statements · **Organiser:** —<br>
**Speakers:** **António Guterres** (UN Secretary-General); **Simon Stiell** (UNFCCC Executive Secretary); **Chris Bowen** (President of Negotiations, Australian Minister for Climate Change and Energy); **Maina Talia** (Tuvalu Climate Minister); **Mohamed Adow** (Power Shift Africa); **Asad Rehman** (Friends of the Earth)<br>
🎥 Video not yet available

## In brief
**UN leaders and the COP31 Presidencies** called the outcome disappointing, but defended the technical results ✅ [1]. It matters because it shows how COP31 will be framed: a political failure with some practical progress 🟡 [2][3].

## Key points
- **António Guterres, UN Secretary-General**, called the outcome "deeply disappointing" and urged immediate action ✅ [1].
- **Simon Stiell, Executive Secretary of the UNFCCC (the UN climate convention)**, cited the just transition agreement and Action Agenda initiatives on electrification, resilience and investment ✅ [1].
- **Chris Bowen, President of Negotiations**, called the outcome "very disappointing" 🟡 [2][3]. According to CNN he added that "a lot of good things" came out of the talks ⚠️ [2].
- **Maina Talia, Tuvalu Climate Minister**, said: "It seems like we are negotiating with the Saudis" ⚠️ according to The Independent [3].
- **Mohamed Adow, director of Power Shift Africa (NGO)**, called COP31 "the worst ever COP" ⚠️ NGO position [2]. **Asad Rehman, Friends of the Earth**, urged governments to come back to the table ⚠️ NGO position [3].

## Quotes
- "There may be no consensus in the negotiating room. There is no ambiguity in the science." — **António Guterres, UN Secretary-General** [1]
- "The world cannot afford a repeat of this moment." — **Simon Stiell, UNFCCC Executive Secretary** [1]
