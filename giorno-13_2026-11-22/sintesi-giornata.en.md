# COP31 — Day 13 · Sunday 22 November 2026
✅ Final version — consolidated on 23/11/2026 at 09:56 (Italian time)<br>
⏱️ Third day of overtime and close of COP31: **the final decision (the "Antalya Pact") was not adopted** ✅ [1][2].

## In brief
- **What happened:** **Murat Kurum, COP31 President**, noted in plenary that there was no consensus on the final decision, which was therefore not adopted ✅ [1][4].
- **Why it matters:** it is the first COP since 2009 (Copenhagen) to close without an overarching political agreement, after the longest conference ever ✅ [1] 🟡 [7][9].
- **What next:** COP31 is closed; open issues move to the technical bodies in Bonn (June 2027) and to COP32 in Addis Ababa ✅ [1].

## Key facts
1. **Murat Kurum, COP31 President and Turkish Environment Minister**, told the plenary there was no agreement on the text and the decisions were not adopted ✅ [1]. He added that "nobody got everything they wanted" ⚠️ according to Xinhua [11].<br>
   🎥 [Full video — COP31 closing plenary (official UN Climate Change channel, 1 h 07')](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y3-yVRkgYBM)
2. **The European Union** raised a formal objection because the text contained no reference to fossil fuels ✅ [1]. **Darragh O'Brien, Irish Climate Minister, on behalf of the EU**, said a climate pact that does not mention fossil fuels is "unconscionable" 🟡 [7][9].
3. **The government of Panama** rejected the text and **the Marshall Islands** did not support it ✅ [1]. **Norway** and **the Least Developed Countries (LDCs, 44 countries)** also objected 🟡 [5][7][10]. According to Climate Home News around 90 countries were opposed ⚠️ [5].
4. **Ghana, on behalf of the African Group (54 countries)**, said it would not block consensus despite gaps on finance, loss and damage and just transition ✅ [1]. **Saudi Arabia, on behalf of the Arab League**, defended the references to carbon capture and storage ✅ [1].
5. **António Guterres, UN Secretary-General**, said he was "deeply disappointed": "There may be no consensus in the negotiating room. There is no ambiguity in the science" ✅ [2]. He asked developed countries to put "dates and dollars" behind the pledge to triple adaptation finance ✅ [2].
6. **Simon Stiell, Executive Secretary of the UNFCCC (the UN climate convention)**, said "the world cannot afford a repeat of this moment" ✅ [1]. He cited as progress the agreement on just transition and the Action Agenda initiatives on electrification, resilience and investment ✅ [1].
7. **Chris Bowen, Australian minister and President of Negotiations**, called the outcome "very disappointing" 🟡 [7][8]. According to NBC News he spoke of "unbridgeable red lines" on fossil fuels ⚠️ [9].

## Negotiations: where things stand
| Topic | What moved | What is stuck | Texts released |
|---|---|---|---|
| Final decision ("cover decision", the conference's political text) | — | Not adopted for lack of consensus ✅ [1] | Last draft, version of 22/11 at 02:00 Antalya: no words on fossil fuels, only a reference to the COP28 "UAE Consensus" ✅ [3] |
| NDCs (national emission-reduction plans) / mitigation | The 35% electrification by 2035 goal was in the rejected draft ✅ [3]; it remains a voluntary pledge | Mitigation work programme rejected; according to Alden Meyer (E3G) Russia and Saudi Arabia blocked it ⚠️ [6] | Deferred to Bonn, June 2027 ⚠️ [6] |
| Finance | — | The rejected draft asked for a pathway to triple adaptation finance by 2035, with no new figure ✅ [3] | — |
| Adaptation / GGA (the global goal on adaptation) | GGA decision adopted according to Alden Meyer (E3G) ⚠️ [6] | No new figure for adaptation finance 🟡 [7][10] | Official text not yet on unfccc.int (checked 23/11) |
| Just transition | Agreement on the just transition mechanism ✅ [1]; "weakened" according to Paul Watkinson ⚠️ [6] | — | Official text not yet on unfccc.int |
| Trade / unilateral measures | The rejected draft said climate measures, "including unilateral ones", should not become trade discrimination ✅ [3] | **Russia** criticised CBAM (the EU carbon border levy) ⚠️ according to Il Sole 24 Ore [10] | — |
